Writing – English Club (đáp án mới)

ENGLISH CLUB

Part 1: You want to join an English club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message.

  1. What is your job? – I'm a student./ A student.

  2. What is your favorite time of year? – Summer./ I like summer.

  3. What is your favorite color? – Blue./ It's blue.

  4. What do you like to do in the evening? – Reading books./ I like reading books.

  5. What is the weather like today? – Sunny and warm./ It's sunny and warm.

Part 2: You want to join the English club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

What are your contacts with English in your daily life?

  • I use English every day at school and when I watch English movies or listen to music. I also chat with some foreign friends online in English.

Part 3: You are talking to other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their question. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer.

A: I didn't study English well when I was at school. I have a private teacher now. Can you tell me about your experience of learning English?

  • I also found English difficult at school, but I improved a lot by watching English movies with subtitles and practicing speaking with friends every day. A private teacher can really help you too.

B: I can read and write in English very well, but I have trouble speaking. How can I improve? Can you give me some advice?

  • I think the best way to improve speaking is to practice every day, even by talking to yourself. You could also join a speaking club or find a language partner online to practice with.

C: It is more important to communicate in a language. We don't have to worry about making any mistakes. What is your opinion?

  • I agree that communication is more important than being perfect. Making mistakes is a normal part of learning, and we should not be afraid to speak just because we are not sure.

Part 4: You are a member of the English club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear Member,

Our Club is going to organize a meeting. We want more people to join our club. How should we organize an event? What should be the theme of this event? We want to attract as many audiences as possible of both the young and older people.

  1. Write an email to your friend who is also a member to talk about your suggestions.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club wants to organize an event to attract more members. I think we should have an English movie night with games. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to your manager about your suggestions and reasons.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the English club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my ideas about the upcoming event mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the club wants to organize an event to attract more members of all ages. I was very excited to hear that news, because I think it is a great opportunity for our club.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I suggest an English movie night, because it is fun and attracts young people. Second, we could also add simple English quiz games with small prizes, since this could attract both young and older members.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
