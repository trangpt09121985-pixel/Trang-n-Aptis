Writing Part 3 – Food, Home Living

WRITING PART 3

Part 3-1 (Food Club): You are a member of a food club. You are talking to 3 other members in the food club chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words

  1. Hi! Welcome to the club. Who did you have dinner with yesterday? and where?

  • I had dinner with my friends at/in a restaurant near my house. It was big and beautiful. We ate seafood and salad. The food was delicious and healthy. I was happy with food and the service here.

  • I had dinner with my friends at/in a restaurant near my house. It was really fun. I could relax after a busy day and share happy moments with my friends. Besides, I could enjoy many kinds of food here. It was really delicious.

  • I had dinner with my best friend at/in a restaurant which was located in the city centre. We ate seafood and salad which were so delicious and healthy. I was satisfied with the food and the service here

  1. Hi! Do you prefer eating out or eating at home?

  • I prefer eating at home because it is healthy and affordable. It helps me improve my health and save money for other important things. Besides, I can share happy moments with my family. I don’t like eating out because it is expensive and noisy.

  1. Should a tax be imposed on unhealthy food?

  • In my opinion, a tax should be imposed on unhealthy food because it is not good for our health. It can cause some health problems such as cancer, obesity and heart diseases.

Part 3-2 (Home living Club): You are a member of a home living club. You are talking to 3 other members in the food club chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words

  1. You have to stay in the bedroom but have to share with your brother. How do you feel?

  • I feel happy because it is interesting and convenient. We can talk and help each other. Besides, we can save money for other important things such as healthcare or education.

  • Hi. My name is Mary. I am a new member. I like your question. I feel happy because it is interesting and convenient. We can talk and help each other. Besides, we can save money for other important things such as healthcare or education.

  1. It is better to live in a big city than in the countryside. Do you agree? Why?

  • I strongly agree with the idea because it is exciting and convenient to live in a big city. People can get good jobs with high salary in big companies and enjoy many kinds of entertainments.

  • I don’t agree with the idea because in the countryside, the air is fresh. The people are friendly and kind. The food is delicious and healthy. A big city is noisy, polluted and expensive.

  1. Nowadays, old buildings are demolished and replaced with modern buildings. I feel we should protect historic buildings. What do you think?

  • I think that old buildings should be demolished and replaced with modern buildings which are safe, convenient and comfortable (with good facilities). However, we should protect historic buildings because young people can learn about the history and the people in the past.

  • I believe that old buildings should be demolished and replaced with modern buildings which are much safer, more convenient and comfortable (with good facilities). However, it is necessary to protect historic buildings because young people can learn about the history and the people in the past.

Các câu có thể giúp tăng số từ

Chào hỏi đơn giản

  • Hi! That’s an interesting question.

  • Hello! Thanks for your question.

  • Hi everyone! Here’s what I think. (hỏi ý kiến cá nhân)

  • That’s a great question!

Chuyển sang ý kiến của mình (với những câu hỏi hỏi ý kiến)

  • Personally, I think…

  • In my opinion,…

  • From my point of view,…

  • As far as I’m concerned,…

  • If you ask me,…

Thể hiện đồng tình trước (với câu hỏi ý kiến)

  • I agree with you to some extent, but…

  • I completely/totally/strongly agree with you because…

  • You make a good point. However,…

  • That’s true, but I also think…

Chống cháy (k hiểu câu hỏi, k trả lời dc - viết và Nói)

  • Hi! My name is Mary. I am a new member. To be honest, this question/topic is not familiar to me so I don’t have any ideas. I will get back to you later
