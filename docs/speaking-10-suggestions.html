Video 4: Practice

  • Lần 1: hiện câu hỏi, rồi hiện câu trả lời, kèm giọng đọc

  • Lần 2,3: hiện chữ và đọc theo

  • Lần 4: cho vài key word, học viên tự nói

  1. Why do you learn English? (độ cần thiết, lợi ích)

Because it is very important in our lives. It helps people get good jobs and communicate with people from different countries. Besides, it helps connect people all over the world.

  1. Why do you travel by bus?

Because it is affordable and good for the environment. It is suitable for students or people with low income

It can help people save money for other things and reduce the number of cars and motorbikes on the road. Therefore, it can reduce air pollution and traffic jams

  1. Why do you learn online?

Because it is really convenient and suitable for busy people. It helps people save time for other things. They can stay at home and learn with a computer. They don’t have to go out. They can learn anywhere at any time

  1. What do you do in your free time?

In my free time, I often read books. It is really useful. It helps me relax after a busy day and broaden my mind. I can update information and learn a lot of things.

Video 5: Practice

  • Lần 1: hiện câu hỏi, rồi hiện câu trả lời, kèm giọng đọc

  • Lần 2,3: hiện chữ và đọc theo

  • Lần 4: cho vài key word, học viên tự nói

  1. What is your hobby?

I love running in the park. It is relaxing and good for my health. It helps me relax after a busy day and stay healthy. It is the best way to keep fit and prevent health problems.

  1. What should we do to protect the environment? Why?

We should plant more trees and use public transport because these actions can help clean the air and reduce the number of cars and motorbikes on the road. Therefore, they can reduce air pollution and traffic jams

  1. What do you do in the evening?

I often watch TV with my family and friends. It is fun and useful. It helps me relax after a busy day and share happy moments with my family. Besides, I can broaden my knowledge of the world.

  1. Why should we encourage children to do volunteer work?

Because it is really useful. It helps children improve many skills such as communication or language skills. They know how to manage their time and communicate with other people. This is really good for their future work and life. They can become more confident and independent. Besides, it can make the world better
