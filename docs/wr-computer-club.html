Writing – Computer Club (đáp án mới)

COMPUTER CLUB

Question 1: You want to join a Computer club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your favourite colour? – Blue./ It's blue.

  2. What is your favourite sport? – Swimming./ I like swimming.

  3. What is your favourite time of the year? – Winter./ I like winter.

  4. What do you like to do in the evening? – Playing games./ I like playing games.

  5. What is the weather like today? – Cloudy and cold./ It's cloudy and cold.

Question 2: You are a new member of the Computer club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When and where do you usually use a computer?

  • I usually use a computer at home in the evening, after I finish my homework. I use it to chat with friends, watch videos and sometimes do some online shopping.

Question 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Kim: I love new technology. Can you please tell me about the last time you used a computer to do something?

  • Last week I used a computer to book train tickets for a trip with my family. It was very quick and convenient, and I did not have to queue at the station.

Marco: My grandmother doesn't know anything about computers? How can she learn about them? What should she do?

  • Your grandmother could start with simple things, like video calls and online games. You could also give her a short lesson every week and be patient, because it takes time to learn something new.

Sylvia: Young people spend too much time using computers. Some say it is not good for them. What is your opinion?

  • I agree that some young people use computers too much. However, if they use it for studying or communicating with friends and family, I think it is not really a bad thing.

Question 4: You are a member of the Computer Club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

We are making some changes to the website because we want more people to join us. As you know, the website at the moment is very simple and we would be interested to hear some ideas about ways to make it more attractive and why you think it would attract more visitors.

Bests, Club Mananger.

  1. Write an email to your friend, who is also a member of this club. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club is planning to redesign the website to attract more members. I think we should add more photos and videos of our activities. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the computer club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming changes to our website mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the website is going to be redesigned to attract more visitors. I was very happy to hear that news, because I think our current website is too simple.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should add more photos and short videos of our club activities, because this will make the website look more attractive. Second, we should promote the website on social networking sites such as Facebook, because it can reach a lot more people.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
