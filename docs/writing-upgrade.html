Mẹo nâng điểm viết

(By Ms Mai Hien)

  1. I like English

  • I am really interested in English which is the most popular language

  • I am really interested in English because it is the most popular language

  • I am really interested in English because it is spoken all over the world

  • I am really interested in English although it is not easy

  • I am really interested in English although it is challenging to learn

  1. I often travel by bus because it is cheap so I can save money. It is suitable for poor students

  • I often travel by bus because it is affordable/economical. Therefore, I can save transportation costs for other things such as healthcare or education. It is suitable for people with limited budgets

  1. Online learning is good for students

  • Online learning is beneficial for students who are busy or have limited budgets.

  1. I really like to go to Halong Bay

  • I love visiting Halong Bay, which is the most travel destinations in Vietnam.
