Tell me about a painting or photo you like.

– I like the photo of my family. It shows my family on the beach. It is beautiful and I like it because it reminds me of my family.

Part 3: Reply to the questions in the chat room. Use 30-40 words per answer.

A: I kept a painting for a long time. Tell me a thing that you have had for a long time.

– Hi. I like your question. It is interesting. I don't think street art is bad. I think it is special and it can make the boring buildings more beautiful and colourful.

Dear members,

The Art Club is organising a talk to the public to attract more attention. We are going to invite an artist to give a talk to the members.
