Debate Club

Part 1: You want to join a debate club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your favorite time of the year?

  2. What is your favorite sport?

  3. Where do you wanna go on holiday?

  4. What is the weather today?

  5. What do you like to do in the evening?

Part 2: You are a new member of the debate club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

What do you usually talk about with your friends?

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the debate club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Sajed: Nowadays there are many ways to follow news. What do you do to know about world issues?

Jo: I am going to have a debate in front of 50 people. I am wondering about how to make a good impression, do you have any advice for me?

Chris: Before making a decision, people should think about two sides of the argument. Do you agree? Why or why not?

I want more people to join our club. But many young people think that debating is not their thing. Do you have any ideas? What are the benefits of debating topical issues and public speaking? Can you give 2 most important benefits and tell me why they are important? I will publish your ideas on our website.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write 120-150 words. Recommended time: 20 minutes.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. (I have been an active member of the club for the last 6 months). I am writing this email to express my ideas regarding the problem mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that young people are not interested in debating. I was truly worried to hear that news because debating is really useful.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. It is advisable for us to have a talk show and invite a famous MC to come and share about the benefits of debating or public speaking. We can offer free debating courses to attract more young people to join our club. It is practical and feasible to carry out. There are two important benefits of debating. First, it can improve communication skill and critical thinking. Second, it can help young people feel more confident.
Hopefully, my ideas will be useful to our club.

Thank you

Feelings: sad – many young people think debating is not their thing (many young people don’t care about debating/ many young people are not interested in debating)

Suggestions:

  • Have a talk show and invite a famous MC to come and share about the benefits of debating. There are two important benefits of debating and public speaking. First, young people feel more confident and they can improve their communication skills. It is good for their life and future jobs. Second, they can improve their critical thinking so they can broaden their mind.

  • Work with local schools and introduce about the benefits/importance of debating.

  • Offer free debating/public speaking courses to attract more young people to join our club
