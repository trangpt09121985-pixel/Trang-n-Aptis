Speaking Part 2 – mẫu câu tả tranh

Speaking Part 2: By Ms. Mai Hien

Tả người

  • In the picture, I can see some people. They are about 20 years old. I think they are friends

  • In the picture, I can see many people

  • In the picture, I can see two people, a man and a woman. They are about 40 years old. I think they are a couple

  • In the picture, I can see two people, a man and a child. The man is about 40 and the child is about 10. I think they are father and son

  • In the picture, I can see a girl. She is about 20 years old. She looks young and beautiful

tả hành động + nơi chốn

  • They are relaxing at home

  • They are relaxing on the beach

  • They are playing sports in the park

  • She is watching movies in the cinema

  • They are eating in the restaurant

  • He is standing on the mountain

  • They are walking in the forest

  • She is standing in the desert

  • They are sitting at the table and talking

  • They are having a meeting

Tả trang phục

  • They are wearing casual clothes

  • They are wearing formal clothes

  • He is wearing a T-shirt and a pair of jeans

  • They are wearing uniform

Tả cảm xúc của người trong tranh

  • They look excited

  • He looks happy

  • She looks focused

Tả đồ vật xung quanh

  • On the table, I can see some food and drink

  • On the wall, I can see some pictures

  • Behind them, I can see some plants and decorations

  • Around them, I can see many trees

  • In the picture, I can see a cat on the floor

Tả thời thiết

  • In the picture, the weather is very nice. It is sunny and perfect for outdoor activities.

  • In the picture, the weather is very hot. The sun is shining.

  • In the picture, the weather is very cold because I can see a lot of snow and the people are wearing warm clothes

tả background

  • In the background of the picture, I can see many people

  • In the background of the picture, I can see mountains and trees

  • In the background of the picture, I can see some buildings

Tả cảm xúc của người miêu tả

  • I like this picture because it reminds me of my childhood. When I was small, I liked playing games with my friends

  • I like this picture because it reminds me of my father. When I was small, I often play football with him

  • I like this picture because it reminds me of my friends. We often go out together.
