Test 12 – Đáp án (Mai Hien)

Aptis speaking (shop, beach, mountain, a conversation)

Part 1: In this part, I am going to ask you three short questions about yourself and your interests. You will have 30 seconds to reply to each question.

  1. What did you do last night?

I went to the cinema with my friends. We saw an action film by Jackie Chan. It’s really interesting. My friend chose the seats early, so we got a great view of the screen. I really want to watch another Jackie Chan movie soon.

  1. What is your favorite season?

I like spring. IT’s beautiful with trees and flowers. The weather is sunny and warm. I love taking a walk in the park during this season because everything looks fresh and colorful. It also makes me feel relaxed and happy.

  1. What is your favorite movie star?

My favourite movie star is Jackie Chan. He is about 50 years old from Hong Hong. He stars in many famous action films. They are really interesting. I have watched most of his movies many times because his action scenes are amazing. He also inspires me to stay fit and work hard.

Part 2: In this part, I'm going to ask you to describe a picture. Then I will ask you two questions about it. You will have 45 seconds for each response.

  1. Describe this picture

  • In the picture, there are two men. They are about 30 years old.

  • They are in a shop.

  • They are looking at a T-shirt

  • They are smiling. I think they are happy with the T-shirt

  • There are a lot of clothes in the shop
  1. Tell me about the last time you went shopping for clothes

Last week I went to a supermarket to buy some clothes. I bought a dress. It was beautiful but expensive. It cost 200 dollars. I liked it. I wore it to my friend's birthday party and received a lot of compliments. Although it was a bit expensive, I felt it was worth the money.

  1. Why do some people dislike going shopping for clothes?

Some people dislike going shopping for clothes because it is time-consuming and tiring. Also, they are not interested in fashion. For example, trying on many clothes in crowded shops can be exhausting. Some people would rather spend that time relaxing at home.

Part 3: In this part, I'm going to ask you to compare two pictures, and I will then ask you two questions about them. You will have 45 seconds for each response.

  1. Describe these 2 pictures

  • The two pictures show people in different places

  • In the first picture, I can see many people on the beach. They are sunbathing and talking. They look relaxed. There are many umbrellas. The water is clear and blue.

  • In the second picture, I can see a girl. She looks young. She is sitting by a lake. She looks calm and relaxed. In front of her, I can see mountains and blue sky.
  1. What are the attractions of visiting 2 places?

  • Both places have different attractions depending on people’s interests.

  • Some people like spending time on the beach because they like swimming and eating seafood. Also, they can share happy moments with family and friends

  • For me, I prefer spending time in the mountain because it is quiet and peaceful. The air is fresh and pleasant. The food is delicious and healthy. Last year, I went hiking in the mountains with my family and we had a wonderful time. I would love to go back there again someday.

  1. What are the benefits of being alone from time to time?

  • There are some benefits of being alone.

  • First, people can relax and do what they want

  • Second, they can focus on their work. For example, I sometimes read a book alone in the evening and it helps me feel calm. I think everyone needs quiet time once in a while.

Part 4: In this part, I'm going to show you a picture and ask you three questions. You will have one minute to think about your answers before you start speaking. You will have two minutes to answer all three questions.

1. Tell me a time when you had an interesting conversation with someone.

I had an interesting conversation with my friend last month. We talked about our future plans and shared many ideas. It was fun and meaningful. We sat in a small coffee shop and talked for almost two hours. I still remember his advice about choosing a career.

2. How did you feel about it?

I felt happy and excited. I learned something new and enjoyed listening to my friend. It also made our friendship stronger than before.

3. What should we do to have a good conversation?

We should listen carefully and respect other people’s opinions. We should also ask questions and speak politely. For example, we should avoid interrupting when someone is talking. This makes the other person feel respected.
