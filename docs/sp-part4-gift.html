Tell me a time when you got a gift/present.

3. As far as I can remember, it was 10 years ago when I was a student

4. I got a gift from my father on my birthday

5. It was a Macbook

6. It was very expensive, about 40 million VND

7. It was really useful

8. I could do many things

9. I could learn online, play games and so on.

10. I really like it

How did you feel? (happy/ excited/ proud of myself/nervous/scared/sad/disappointed)

  1. I felt happy. I could relax after a busy day and share happy moments with my family and friends. (tinh thần)

  2. I could learn a lot of things and broaden my mind about the world (kiến thức). I knew how cook for my family.

  3. I felt very happy because it was really useful. It helped me a lot

  4. I felt so excited because I had never been there

  5. On that day, I had many different feelings

  6. At that time,

  7. At first, I felt so nervous/excited/scared (because I had never been in this situation before)

  8. But after that I felt so happy and proud of myself (because I had tried my best/because I had done a good thing)

  9. Although this happened a long time ago, everything is still in my mind and I will never forget.
