Writing P4 – khung chung & giải pháp (bản 1)

Writing P4

(khung chung cho các dạng bài và 10 giải pháp dễ áp dụng nhất)

  1. Viết cho bạn

Hi Jane,

I just heard that (thông tin trong bài). I think we should ………… What do you think? Let’s talk about it over lunch.

See you later

VD:

Hi Jane,

I just heard that we are going to organize a sports event. I think we should organize in summer when students have their vacation. It is better for us to organize in Thonng Nhat Park because it is big and convenient. What do you think? Let’s talk about it over lunch.

See you later

  1. Viết cho quản lý

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming (event/meeting/plan/exhibition..) / problem mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that (………….). To be honest, I was so happy (sad/worried/disappointed) to hear that news.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, (idea 1 - giải thích). Second, (idea 2 - giải thích).

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards

VD:

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming event mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club is going to plan to encourage young people to cook at home. To be honest, I was so happy to hear that news.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should have a talk show and invite a master chef to come and talk about the benefits of cooking at home. It is practical and feasible to carry out. Second, we can offer a free cooking course for people who want to learn how to cook. It is the most effective in the long term.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards

Các giải pháp chống cháy – kèm giải thích ngắn gọn, dễ

  1. We should have a face-to-face or an online meeting for members to express their ideas directly. It is fast and easy to carry out

  2. We should ask for help from an expert who has experience to deal with this. It is the best way

  3. We should have a talk show/workshop and invite a famous person to come and talk about the benefits of (playing sports/reading books …). It is practical and feasible to carry out.

  4. We should educate people about the importance of (art/environment..). It is the most effective in the long term

  5. We should ask for help from local governments and organizations. It is fast and practical to carry out.

  6. We should work with the local government and local schools to introduce about the …

  7. We should promote the event on Facebook because it can reach more people.

  8. We should offer free (tickets/courses/training..) for (local people). It is practical and easy to do.

  9. We should not change anything because it can cause a lot of inconvenience (gây ra nhiều sự bất tiện) for everybody and it may discourage members. (làm nản các thành viên)

  10. We should set clear rules (đề ra quy định rõ ràng) about these behaviors (hành vi). It is the most effective in the long term

  • We should /can..

  • It is better for us to …

  • It is advisable for us to…

  • I am thinking about ….

  • I suggest + V-ing

  • I suggest we should…
