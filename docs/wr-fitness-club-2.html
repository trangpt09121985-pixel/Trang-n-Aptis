Writing – Fitness Club 2 (đáp án mới)

FITNESS CLUB

Part 1: You want to join the Fitness club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your favorite food? – Fried rice./ I like fried rice.

  2. What is your favorite color? – Blue./ It's blue.

  3. Where did you go last night? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  4. What do you do on the weekend? – Playing football./ I like playing football.

  5. What is the most popular sport in your country? – Football./ Football is the most popular.

Part 2: You want to join the Fitness club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Tell us about the last time you had to run somewhere.

  • Last week, I had to run to the bus stop because I was late for work. I was very tired when I finally arrived, but I managed to catch the bus.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: How did you hear about the Health club?

  • I heard about the Health club from a close friend who is already a member. She told me that the facilities are good and the instructors are very friendly and helpful.

B: What do you want to achieve when joining the club?

  • I want to lose some weight and become healthier when I join the club. I would also like to make new friends who enjoy exercising and can encourage me to keep going.

C: Do you have any opinions about the facilities in the gym?

  • I think the facilities in the gym are quite good, but some machines are old and need to be repaired. Apart from that, the gym is clean and has enough space for everyone.

Part 4: You are a member of the Health club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear Member,

The club's instructors will be absent for the next 2 weeks. You can still use the club's equipment, but there won't be any instructor support. Please kindly understand this situation.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your feelings and opinions. 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our instructors will be away for two weeks. I think we should follow online workout videos to keep exercising safely. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your feelings and suggestions. 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the fitness club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, our instructors will be absent for the next two weeks. I was a little worried to hear that news, since I usually need some guidance during my workouts.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some suggestions. First, the club could share some simple online workout videos for members to follow safely during this time. Second, experienced members could help and support beginners, since this is a fast and easy way to solve the problem.

Hopefully, my suggestions will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
