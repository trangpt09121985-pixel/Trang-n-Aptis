Writing P4 – Forms – formal letters

Part 4: You are a member of the Computer Club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

We are making some changes to the website because we want more people to join us. As you know, the website at the moment is very simple and we would be interested to hear some ideas about ways to make it more attractive and why you think it would attract more visitors.

Bests,

Club Mananger.

  1. Write an email to your friend, who is also a member of this club. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words

  2. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dạng bài tổ chức sự kiện, lên kế hoạch, sự thay đổi. (nên dùng chatGPt tạo form riêng)

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to share my opinions about the upcoming (plan/event/change) mentioned in the recent email from our club.

As stated in the email, (thông tin trong đề). I was truly delighted to hear the news because I believe that this plan will be very useful and beneficial for both our club and the community.

To make this plan more successful, I would like to share a few ideas.

* Idea 1 + reason

* Idea 2 + reason

I hope this plan will help connect our members and strengthen our community.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to hearing your thoughts.

Mary.

VD:

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to share my opinions about the upcoming changes (to our club’s website) mentioned in the recent email from our club.

As stated in the email, the club is planning to (improve/make some changes to) the website. I was truly delighted to hear the news because I believe this plan will be very useful and beneficial for both our club and the community.

To make this plan more successful, I would like to share a few ideas. First, we should post more photos and videos of our activities because they can make the website more attractive and show visitors what we do. Second, it is advisable for us to update the website more often with the latest news.

I hope this plan will help connect our members and strengthen our community.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to hearing your thoughts.

Part 4: You are a member of the Technology Club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear member,

Members are active online by posting and commenting on posts. However, some members are commenting negative (tiêu cực) things about others. The postings on our system are anonymous (hệ thống ẩn danh) so we are thinking about changing the system to identify (định danh) who comments. What do you think? Do you have any alternative method? Provide your suggestions and

reasons.

From the manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend who is also a member to talk about your suggestions.

  2. Write an email to your manager about your suggestions and reasons.

* Idea 1 + reason

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to share my opinions about the problem mentioned in the recent email from our club.

As stated in the email, (thông tin trong đề). I was really disappointed to hear the news because this problem can badly affect club members and the community.

To address the problem, I would like to share a few ideas. I think we should not change the present system because it can discourage members to share their ideas. Instead, I suggest setting certain rules for online behaviors. For example, we can block the account that break the rules

I hope that we can fix the problem soon

DẠng bài 2 options

Part 4: You are a member of the Power Fitness club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear Member,

Unfortunately, in order to cut costs, we are forced to reduce the facilities that we can offer our members. Some members have suggested that we close the club shop, where we sell sports equipment. Another suggestion is to close the cafe selling sandwiches and soft drinks. We would be interested to hear what you think. Please write to the manager with your suggestion and your reason for suggesting it.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend what the club should do and why. Write about 50 words.

  2. Write an email to the manager of the club. Say what you think the club should do and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to share my opinions about the upcoming (plan/event) mentioned in the recent email from our club.

As stated in the email, (thông tin trong đề). I was truly delighted/a little sad to hear the news and I think that most of the members share my feelings.

Among the two options, I believe that the (first/second) option is the better choice because

* Idea 1 + reason

* Idea 2 + reason

I hope my ideas will be useful for our club.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to hearing your thoughts.

Mary.

VD:

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to share my opinions about the upcoming plan mentioned in the recent email from our club.

As stated in the email, our club is going to reduce facilities to cut costs. I was a little sad to hear the news and I think that most of the members share my feelings.

Among the two options, I believe that the first option is the better choice because members can buy equipment from other shops nearby. There are many shops that sell sports items around our club. Moreover, the café is the place where members can socialize and relax after their workout. It’s really important.

I hope my ideas will be useful for our club.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to hearing your thoughts.

Mary.
