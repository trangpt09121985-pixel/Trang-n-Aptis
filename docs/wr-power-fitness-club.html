Writing – Power Fitness Club (đáp án mới)

POWER FITNESS CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a power fitness club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Which sport do you like to play the most? – Football./ I like football.

  2. What did you do last night? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  3. What do you do on the weekend? – Playing football./ I like playing football.

  4. Do you like shopping? – Yes, I do./ Yes, I like shopping.

  5. What do you usually do in the morning? – Check my emails./ I usually check my emails.

Part 2: You are a new member of the power fitness club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

What do you want to do and when are you free to exercise?

  • I want to try different sports and get fitter this year. I am usually free to exercise in the evening after work, from around 6 to 8 pm.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Amy: Hi. Welcome to the club. I joined because I love sports and I want to try out as many as possible. Why did you join?

  • Hi Amy, thanks for the warm welcome! I joined the club because I want to get fitter and try some new sports. I'm really looking forward to meeting everyone here.

Philip: I am very busy and I don't have a lot of time to keep fit. Can you give me some advice?

  • I understand how busy life can be. I suggest doing short 20-minute workouts a few times a week instead of long sessions, and try to exercise at a time that fits your schedule.

Steve: More and more people are becoming interested in keeping fit. What do you think the reason for this is?

  • I think more people are interested in keeping fit because they want to stay healthy and reduce stress from busy modern life. Social media also makes fitness look more popular nowadays.

Part 4: You are a member of the Power Fitness club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear Member,

Unfortunately, in order to cut costs we are forced to reduce the facilities that we can offer our members. Some members have suggested that we close the club shop, where we sell sports equipment. Another suggestion is to close the cafe selling sandwiches and soft drinks. We would be interested to hear what you think. Please write to the manager with your suggestion and your reason for suggesting it.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend what the club should do and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How are you? I just heard that our club needs to cut costs, so it might close the shop or the cafe. I think we should close the shop instead. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Say what you think the club should do and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the power fitness club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the club needs to cut costs by closing either the shop or the cafe. I was a little sad to hear that news, but I understand that it is necessary.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some suggestions. First, I think we should close the shop, because most members buy sports equipment outside the club anyway. Second, we should keep the cafe, since many members like to relax and chat there after training.

Hopefully, my suggestions will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
