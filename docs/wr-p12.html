  2. How many people are there in your family? – Four people/ There are four people.

  3. What did you do yesterday? – I stayed at home.

  4. How do you go to school? – By bus./ I go to school by bus.

  5. What’s your hobby? – I love reading books.

  6. What do you like to do in the evening? - I like to watch TV.

  7. What is your favorite device? - I love my iPhone.

  8. Where would you like to live? – I would like to live in China. / In China.

  9. Do you often use Facebook? – Yes, I do/ I use Facebook every day.

  10. What do you do? – A teacher/ I am a teacher.

  11. Where do you live? – In Vietnam/ I live in Vietnam.

  12. What do you do to keep fit? – I play sports.

  13. What’s your favourite kind of music? – I love pop music.

  14. What do you often do in your free time? – I read books.

  15. What is your surname? – My surname is Nguyen.

  16. What is your favourite animal? – I love cats.

  17. What time do you get up every day? – At 6AM.

  18. What language do you speak? – I speak Vietnamese.

  19. Do you like to drink coffee? – yes, I do.

  20. What’s your favourite color? – I love blue.

  21. What are you wearing today? – A beautiful dress/ I am wearing a dress.

  22. What’s the weather like today? – Sunny/ It’s sunny.

  24. How did you come here? – By car/ I came here by car.

WRITING PART 2 (20-30 words)

  1. What do you often do in your free time? (20-30 words) (Book Club)

  • In my free time, I often read books. It is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after work and broaden my mind.

  • In my free time, I often read books. It is really enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after a busy day and broaden my mind.

  1. Please tell me the last time you listened to music. (Music Club)

  • Last week I listened to music. It was enjoyable and useful. It helped me relax after a busy day and share happy moments with my friends.

  1. Please tell me about the last time you went to the/a museum? (20-30 words) (Museum Club)

  • Last week I went to HMC Museum. It was enjoyable and useful. It helped me relax and understand more about Uncle Ho.

  1. Do you usually watch TV? (Television Club)

  • Yes. I watch TV every day. It is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after a busy day and broaden my mind. Besides, I can share happy moments with my family.

  1. Please tell us about the days and times you can attend courses and what you would like to study? (College Club)

  • I would like to study English. I can attend the course on Monday mornings and Friday afternoons or at the weekend.

  1. When and where do you use a computer? (20-30words) (Computer Club)

  • I often use my computer in the evening. I learn English online. It’s really convenient because I can stay at home and learn with a computer.

  1. What do you usually use your laptop for? (Technology Club)

  • I usually use my laptop for work and study. For example, I learn English online. It’s really convenient because I can stay at home and learn with a computer.

  1. Do you like science? (science club)

  • Yes. I find a lot of interest in science because it is really interesting and useful. It helps me understand more about the world around me.

Like = be interested in, enjoy, be fond of, keen on, find a lot of interest in …

  1. Describe where you live (Home Living Club)

  • I live in a house in a big city. It is small but very nice. There are five rooms in my house. I like my bedroom best because I can do many things there.

  1. Why are you interested in travel? (Travel Club)

  • I am interested in travel because it is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after busy days and broaden my mind. Besides, I can share happy moments with my family and friends.

  1. Why did you decide to join the club? (Cooking Club)

  • I decided to join the club because I like cooking and I want to learn new dishes. Besides, I want to meet members who have the same hobbies.

  • I decided to join the club because I am really interested in cooking and I would like to learn new dishes. Besides, I wish to meet members who share the same hobbies.
