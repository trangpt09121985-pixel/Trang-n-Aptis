Writing – English Club 2 (đáp án mới)

ENGLISH CLUB

Part 1: You want to join an English club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Who's your favourite person? – My mother./ It's my mother.

  2. How do you travel around your area? – By car./ I travel by car.

  3. What's your first language? – Vietnamese./ It's Vietnamese.

  4. What kind of music do you like listening to? – Pop music./ It's pop music.

  5. What time do you often go to bed? – At 10 pm./ I go to bed at 10 pm.

Part 2: You are a new member of the English club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

What do you usually use the internet for?

  • I usually use the Internet to learn online, watch movies, listen to music and play games. Sometimes, I use the Internet to chat with my friends.

  • Every day, I use the Internet for work and online study. Sometimes, I use it to listen to music with my family, which helps me relax.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: I usually spend 6 hours a day studying English. How do you learn English?

  • I usually spend only 2 hours learning English. I learn English by watching movies and reading books in English. Sometimes, I also practice speaking with English-speaking friends I meet online.

  • I join an English club where I can practice speaking English with people from different countries. It's really useful for me because it helps me improve my English fast and effectively.

B: English is a popular language in the world. What are your thoughts on this idea?

  • I totally agree with this idea. English is the international language which is spoken all over the world. It helps us communicate with people from different countries and get good jobs.

C: When do you usually use English?

  • I usually use English in class, where I have to speak English with teachers and friends. Sometimes, I use English to speak with some of my friends who come from different countries.

Part 4: You are a member of the English club. Nowadays, more and more new words and phrases are being added to the language, so many people have strong reactions to this issue. Some people gladly accept them, while others argue that there should be rules for language.

  1. Write an email to your friend to express your views and explain why so many people react like this. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Mary. How's it going? Did you get the latest email about new words and phrases? I think new words are interesting and fun. Some people have strong reactions to this issue because they are old and they don't like changes. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

Catch you later.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club to express your views and explain why so many people react like this. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I have been an active member of the club for six months. I am writing this email regarding the issue mentioned in the recent announcement from our club.

I have been informed that nowadays more and more new words and phrases are added to the language, so many people have strong reactions to this issue. Some people like new words and phrases because they are interesting and fun, and language would become boring without them. Others, especially older people, do not like the changes.

In my opinion, we can accept some new words and phrases because they can make the language more colourful. However, there should be certain rules, and we should not accept words that can cause bad feelings.

Hopefully, my ideas will be helpful to our club.

Best regards
