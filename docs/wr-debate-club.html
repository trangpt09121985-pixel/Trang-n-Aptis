DEBATE CLUB

Question 1: You want to join a debate club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your favorite time of the year? – Summer./ I like summer.

  2. What is your favorite sport? – Football./ I like football.

  3. Where do you wanna go on holiday? – Da Nang./ I want to go to Da Nang.

  4. What is the weather today? – Sunny and warm./ It's sunny and warm.

  5. What do you like to do in the evening? – Watching movies./ I like watching movies.

Question 2: You are a new member of the debate club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

What do you usually talk about with your friends?

  • With my friends, I usually talk about school, music and sports. We also talk about films we have watched and share our opinions about the news.

Question 3: You are communicating with other members of the debate club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Sajed: Nowadays there are many ways to follow news. What do you do to know about world issues?

  • I usually follow the news on social media and news apps on my phone. I also watch the news on TV every evening with my family to know what is happening around the world.

Jo: I am going to have a debate in front of 50 people. I am wondering about how to make a good impression, do you have any advice for me?

  • Congratulations on your debate! I think you should practice in front of a mirror first and speak slowly and clearly. Try to look at the audience and stay calm, and it will help you make a good impression.

Chris: Before making a decision, people should think about two sides of the argument. Do you agree? Why or why not?

  • Yes, I agree with you. Before making a decision, it is important to think about both sides of an argument. This way, we can avoid mistakes and make a fairer, more reasonable decision.

Question 4: You are a member of the debate club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear Member,

I want more people to join our club. But many young people think that debating is not their thing. Do you have any ideas? What are the benefits of debating topical issues and public speaking? Can you give 2 most important benefits and tell me why they are important? I will publish your ideas on our website.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write about 50 words. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club wants more young people to join. I think we should organize some fun debate games and invite guest speakers to make it more interesting. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the president of the club. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write 120-150 words. Recommended time: 20 minutes.

Dear President,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the debate club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my ideas about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, many young people think that debating is not their thing. I was a bit worried to hear that news, because I think debating is very useful for everyone.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, debating helps us improve our public speaking skills and confidence, which are very useful for our studies and future jobs. Second, it helps us think more critically, because we learn to consider different sides of an issue before giving an opinion.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
