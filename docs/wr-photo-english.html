Writing Practice: Photography, English

Part 4: You are a member of the photography club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

Last year, the club held a photo contest (cuộc thi ảnh), but there was only one category (hạng mục). This year, the contest will be divided into (chia thành) several categories, including women, men and children under 16 years old.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

How’s it going? I just heard about the upcoming photo contest. This year the contest will be divided into several categories including women, men, and children under 16. I think this change is good/necessary because it can attract more people, especially children. What do you think? Let’s talk about over lunch.

See you later.

  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I have been an active member of the club for the last six months. I am writing this email to express my ideas regarding the upcoming photo contest.

I have been informed that this year the contest will be divided into several different categories, including women, men and children under 16 years old. Honestly, I was truly delighted to hear that news and I believe that most of the members share my feelings.

In my opinion, this change is necessary because it can make the contest more interesting and suitable for many people/people of all ages. Therefore, it can attract more people, especially young people. Besides, the contest will be fair for everyone. We can promote the event on social networking sites such as Facebook because it can reach more people. It is fast and easy to carry out.

I hope that my ideas will be helpful to our club.

Best regards, (Thank you)

Mary.

Yours sincerely,

Yours faithfully,

Part 4: You are a member of the English club. Nowadays, more and more new words and phrases (cụm từ) are being added (cho vào) to the language, so many people have strong reactions to this issue (có phản ứng mạnh đối với vấn đề). Some people gladly accept (vui vẻ chấp nhận) them, while others argue (tranh luận) that there should be rules for language.

  1. Write an email to your friend to express your views and explain why so many people react like this. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

How’s it going? I just heard that people have different reactions to new words and phrases which are added to the language. I think we should gladly accept these words and phrases because language can change over time. What do you think? Let’s talk about it over lunch.

See you later.

  1. Write an email to the manager of the club to express your views and explain why so many people react (phản ứng) like this. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I have been an active member of the club for the last six months. I am writing this email to express my ideas regarding the new words and phrases mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that people have different reactions to new words and phrases. Some people do not like these words and phrases while others accept these. In my opinion, we should gladly accept these words because languages can change over time together with the development of society. There are new things and we need new words to talk about them. However, we can add them to dictionaries so people can understand and use them correctly. Some people have strong reactions because they want to keep traditional language and they do not like changes.

Best regards,

Mary.
