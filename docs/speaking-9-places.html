9 places – từ vựng địa điểm

Từ vựng cho chủ đề hay xuất hiện trong Aptis Speaking

(đặc biệt trong Part 3)

  1. Địa điểm:

  1. Hometown, a famous place (tên, ở đâu, ntn, hoạt đôngj, không khí, con người, đồ ăn, cảm xúc)

  • I am going to talk about my hometown/a famous place in VN…. It’s Halong Bay, in Quang Ninh, not far from HN, about 100km. It is stunning and famous. It is one of the most popular travel destinations in Vietnam. Here we can swim in the sea, eat seafood and climb the mountain. The air is fresh. The people are friendly and kind. The food is delicious and cheap. I really like Halong.

  1. The countryside/ rural area/ mountains/ mountainous area/ small communities (yên tĩnh, không khí, con người, đồ ăn)

  • I like to live in the countryside because it is quiet and peaceful. The air is fresh and good for my health. The people are friendly and kind. They always help other people. The food is delicious, healthy and cheap so I can save money. However, it is boring.

  1. City (nhộn nhịp, tiện, tìm việc tốt, nhiều loại hình giải trí, chăm sóc sk tốt, gd tốt, ồn, ô nhiễm)

  • I like to live in a big city because it is exciting and convenient. Here I can get a good job and enjoy many kinds of entertainment. Besides, I can enjoy good healthcare and education. However, it is noisy and polluted. It is not good for my health.

  1. Beach (nhộn nhịp, bơi, ăn hải sản, tắm nắng, đi dạo…)

  • I prefer spending time on the beach because it is really exciting. I love to swim in the sea and eat seafood. Besides, I like sunbathing and walking along the beach.

  1. Mountain (yên tĩnh, leo núi, khám phá, ngắm cảnh đẹp)

  • I like to spend time in the mountain because it is quiet and peaceful. The air is fresh and pleasant. I love to climb the mountain and explore nature. I can enjoy beautiful scenery with green trees and many kinds of flowers.

  1. Indoors (learn, work, relax – yên tĩnh, tập trung, thoải mái, tốt sk, k thích outdoor phụ thuộc thời tiết)

  • I like to (learn/work/relax..) indoors because it is quiet (and peaceful) so I can focus on my work/study. Besides, I feel more comfortable. I don’t like working/studying outdoors because it depends on the weather. If the weather is hot, I will feel tired.

  1. Outdoors (kk trong lành, gặp mn, cải thiện giao tiêp)

  • I like to work/study/learn outdoors because I can enjoy fresh air. It is good for my health. Besides, I can meet people so I can improve communication skills.

  1. Office (yên tĩnh, tập trung cv, thoải mái, k thích lv ở nhà máy, xưởng)

  • I like to work in an office because it is quiet so I can focus on my work. Besides, I feel more comfortable so I can work better. It is good for my health. I don’t like working in a factory (workshop) because it noisy and polluted. Besides, the work is hard (physical demanding). For example, people have to stand for long hours. It is not good for our health

  1. Factory/workshop (nhộn nhịp, gặp mn. ồn, ô nhiễm, cv nặng nhọc, k tốt cho sk)

  • I like to work in a factory because it is exciting. I can meet and talk to many people.

  • I don’t like working in a factory (workshop) because it noisy and polluted. Besides, the work is hard (physical demanding). For example, people have to stand for long hours. It is not good for our health
