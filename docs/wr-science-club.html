Writing – Science Club (đáp án mới)

SCIENCE CLUB

Part 1: You want to join the Science club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your job? – I'm an engineer./ An engineer.

  2. Where did you go last night? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  3. What is your favorite color? – Blue./ It's blue.

  4. What do you like to do in the evening? – Reading books./ I like reading books.

  5. What is the weather like today? – Sunny and warm./ It's sunny and warm.

Part 2: You want to join the Science club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When and where do you usually read science books?

  • I usually read science books at home in the evening, after finishing my work. I especially enjoy reading about space and new technology on weekends.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: I have been interested in science since I was a child because I was lucky when I had a wonderful teacher. How about you? What do you remember about studying science at school?

  • I remember my science teacher doing fun experiments in class, which made me very interested in the subject. Since then, I have always enjoyed learning how things work in the world around us.

B: My friends like watching scientific programmes while I prefer to read science books. How about you? Which do you prefer to do?

  • I also enjoy reading science books more than watching scientific programmes. I think books explain things in more detail, and I can read at my own pace and think about the ideas.

C: Some people think technology has a negative effect on our lives. What do you think?

  • I agree that technology can have a negative effect, for example making people less active. However, I think the benefits, such as easier communication and access to information, are much greater.

Part 4: You are a member of the Science club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear member,

Next month, our club will hold a science exhibition for the public in our local area. What kinds of things do you think we should include? How can we encourage both adults and children to come in? Should we charge an entry fee or give customers for free?

  1. Write an e-mail to your friend. Write about your feelings and what you are planning to do. Write 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club is planning a science exhibition next month. I think we should include fun hands-on experiments for children. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an e-mail to the manager of the club. Write about your feelings and what you would like to do. Write 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the science club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my ideas about the upcoming exhibition mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, our club will hold a science exhibition for the public next month. I was very excited to hear that news, because it is a great chance to share our love of science.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I suggest including fun, hands-on experiments, because this will attract both children and adults. Second, I think the exhibition should be free of charge, since a fee may discourage some families from coming.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
