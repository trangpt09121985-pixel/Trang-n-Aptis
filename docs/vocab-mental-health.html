  1. sức khoẻ - Playing sports/eating at home/ eating healthy food/ Fast food

  1. Picture 4 – sức khoẻ - Playing sports/eating at home/ eating healthy food/ Fast food

Good

  • It good for my health

  • Stay healthy

  • Keep fit

  • It helps me stay healthy and keep fit

  • Prevent health problems such as cancer, obesity or heart diseases

  • Provide nutrition and vitamins which are important for our body

  1. Picture 5: (hình 1 người chơi thể thao)

  • I like playing sports because it is good for my health.

  • It helps me stay healthy, keep fit and prevent health problems.

  1. Picture 6: (hình 1 người đang ăn cá, trứng, rau, salad)

  • I like eating healthy food because it is good for my health.

  • It provides nutrition and vitamins which are important for our body.

  • Besides, it helps me stay healthy, keep fit and prevent health problems

  1. Picture 7: (Hình 1 người đang ăn ở nhà)

  • I like eating at home because it is good for my health.

  • It helps me stay healthy, keep fit and prevent health problems.

Bad

  • Harmful to our health

  • Fast food is harmful to our health

  • Cause some health problems

  • Cause some health problems such as cancer or obesity

  • It contains a lot of oil and sugar

  • It contains a lot of oil and sugar which are not good for our health

  • It is the leading cause of obesity

  • We should not eat fast food because it is harmful to our health. It can cause some health problems such as cancer and obesity because it contains a lot of oil and sugar which are not good for our health. It is the leading cause of obesity.
