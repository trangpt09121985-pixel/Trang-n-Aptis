Từ vựng chủ đề tiền bạc

Từ vựng chủ đề: tiền bạc, kiến thức

Words

  • Cheap, affordable, expensive, economical

  • Travelling by bus is affordable

  • Online learning is affordable

  • People with low income

  • People with limited budgets

  • It is affordable for students or people with low income

  • Life in big cities is expensive

  • Save money

  • I can save money for other things

  • Waste money

  • Travelling by air is expensive. It wastes money

  • Earn money

  • People can earn more money

  • I often travel by bus because it is affordable for students or people with low income. It helps me save money for other things

  • I don’t like travelling by air because it is expensive. It wastes a lot of money

  • I prefer to eat at home because it is affordable for people with low income. It helps me save money for other things

  • I prefer to watch movies at home because it is economical for people with low income. It helps me save money for other things

Practice

  • How do you often travel around your city? (Cho 30 giây để trả lời). Sau đó hiện 30 giây hiện đáp án để học viên tham khảo “I often travel by bus because it is affordable for students or people with low income. It helps me save money for other things. However, sometimes I go by car because it is fast and more convenient”. Sau đó lại cho 30 giây để cho học viên tự nói

  • Which do you prefer? Travelling by train or by air? (cho 45 giây để trả lời). Sau đó hiện 45 giây hiện đáp án để học viên tham khảo “I prefer to travel by train because it is affordable for people with low income. It helps me save money for other things. I don’t like to travel by air because it is expensive. It wastes money”. Sau đó lại cho 45 giây để cho học viên tự nói

  • Where do you prefer to eat? (cho 45 giây để trả lời) Sau đó hiện 45 giây hiện đáp án để học viên tham khảo “I prefer to eat at home because it is affordable for people with low income. It helps me save money for other things. However, sometimes I like to eat in a restaurant because the food is delicious. Sau đó lại cho 45 giây để cho học viên tự nói

  • Where do you prefer to watch movies? (cho 45 giây để trả lời). Sau đó hiện 45 giây hiện đáp án để học viên tham khảo “I prefer to watch movies at home because it is affordable for people with low income. It helps me save money for other things. Besides, I can share happy moment with my family”
