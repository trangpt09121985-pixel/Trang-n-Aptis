Phần I — Từ vựng theo chủ đề

  1. Tinh thần (Mental well-being) (mental health)

  • Fun, enjoyable, interesting, relaxing, amazing

  • Relax after work

  • Relax after a busy/long day

  • Reduce stress

  • Share happy moments with my family and friends

  • Forget all the troubles

VD1: What do you do in your free time?

  • In my free time, I often play games with my friends. It is fun. I can relax after work and share happy moments with my friends. Sometimes I play sports in the park near my house. It’s relaxing.

VD2: What kind of music do you like?

  • I like pop music. I often listen to music with my friends at the weekend. It is enjoyable. I can relax after work and forget all the troubles.

  1. Sức khỏe thể chất (Physical health)

  • Healthy, good for my health, strong, active, harmful to

  • Healthy food: meat, fish, eggs, vegetables, fruits..

  • Provide nutrition and vitamins which are important for our body

  • Improve my health

  • Stay active

  • Keep fit

  • Prevent health problems

  • Not good for our health

  • Cause some health problems such as cancer, obesity, heart disease, eye strain, headache

  • Fast food is the leading cause of obesity.

VD1: What’s your hobby?

  • I love playing sports. I often play sports with my friends at the weekend. It is fun and good for my health. I can relax after work and share happy moments with my friends. Besides, I can improve my health, keep fit and prevent health problems.

VD2: Which do you prefer? (healthy food – fast food)

  • I prefer healthy food because it is good for my health. It provides nutrition and vitamins which are important for our body. I don’t like fast food because it is harmful to our health. It can cause some health problems such as cancer and obesity. It is the leading cause of obesity.

  1. Chi phí (Cost / money)

  • Cheap, expensive, affordable, economical, reasonable, suitable for

  • People with low income

  • People with limited budgets

  • Save money (for other things)

  • Save money for other important things such as healthcare, education and so on

  • Money (= transportation costs, living costs)

  • Reduce living costs

VD: How do you travel around?

  • I travel by bus because it is affordable and suitable for people with low income. I can save money for other things.

VD2: Fast food is becoming more and more popular. What do you think?

  • I agree with the idea. Fast food is becoming more and more popular because it is fast and convenient. Besides, it is affordable and suitable for people with low income. People can save money for other things. (However, it is not good for our health. It can cause some health problems. It is the leading cause of obesity.)
