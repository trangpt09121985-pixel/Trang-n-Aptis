Câu chuyện 1: Ngày tôi đi thi Aptis

Last year, I wanted to change myself, so I decided to take the Aptis test. Before the test, I was very busy and often in a hurry. The test was difficult but I tried my best. Every day, I practised speaking with AI. On the test day, I went to Hanoi to take the test. I didn’t know the way so I asked a man. One week later, I got good news: I passed the exam! I felt happy and proud of myself because I had tried my best
