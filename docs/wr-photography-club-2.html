Writing – Photography Club 2 (đáp án mới)

PHOTOGRAPHY CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a photography club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Where do you live? – In Hanoi./ I live in Hanoi.

  2. Are you married? – Yes, I am./ I am married.

  3. How long have you studied English? – 2 months./ For 2 months.

  4. What do you like to do in your free time? – I always watch TV./ Watching TV.

  5. What did you do last night? – I practised some English lessons./ I studied English.

Part 2: You are a new member of the photography club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Tell me about the last time you took a photo.

  • The last time I took a photo was yesterday. I took some nice photos with my best friend when we had a trip to Ho Guom Lake.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Some people usually upload their pictures to their laptop to store them. Where do you store your photos?

  • I usually store my photos on my laptop because it is convenient and easy for me to look at them when I want to recall happy moments with my best friends and my relatives.

B: Sometimes, I have no idea what to take photos of. Can you share some tips to help photographers get more inspiration for taking interesting pictures?

  • I think photographers can get more inspiration for taking interesting pictures by using the Internet, since a lot of people post videos and pictures there that can spark new ideas.

C: There are many skills that a photographer needs to have. What do you think about the factors that make photographers successful?

  • I think photographers need to be creative and hard-working to become successful, since these qualities help them come up with interesting ideas and create amazing photos. As a result, more people will get to know their work.

Part 4: You are a member of the photography club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

Last year, the club held a photo contest, but there was only one category. This year, the contest will be divided into several categories, including women, men and children under 16 years old.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club's photo contest will now have separate categories for women, men and children this year. I think this is a great change. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming contest mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that this year's contest will be divided into separate categories for women, men and children under 16. To be honest, I was very happy to hear that news, since it makes the competition fairer for everyone.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should promote the new contest on social networking sites, especially Facebook, because it can reach more people and attract more entries. Second, we should offer free entry for children under 16, as this is a practical way to encourage young members to join.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
