Phần I — Từ vựng theo chủ đề

Mỗi chủ đề gồm: tính từ thường dùng, cụm từ/collocation hữu ích, các chủ đề câu hỏi thường áp dụng, và đoạn văn/câu trả lời mẫu (đã gộp các bản trùng nhau trong tài liệu gốc).

1. Tinh thần (Mental health)

Tính từ: fun, enjoyable, interesting, relaxing

Cụm từ hữu ích:

  • relax after a busy day at work and reduce stress

  • share happy moments with family and friends

  • forget all the troubles

Cấu trúc gợi ý: I can …. / It helps/allows me ….

“I like travelling. It is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after a long/busy/hard-working day at work and reduce stress. Besides, I can share happy moments with family and friends. Also, travelling allows me to forget all the troubles.”

2. Sức khỏe thể chất (Physical health)

Tính từ: healthy, active (năng động), strong, harmful to (có hại cho), not good for

Cụm từ hữu ích:

  • eat healthy food / drink enough water / eat more vegetables and fruits

  • provide nutrition and vitamins which are important for our body: cung cấp dưỡng chất và vitamin quan trọng cho cơ thể

  • improve (my) health: tăng cường sk

  • stay active/healthy : duy trì sự năng động/khoẻ mạnh

  • burn calories: đốt calo

  • keep fit: (giữ dáng)

  • prevent health problems (ngăn chặn các vấn đề về sk)

  • cause some health problems (gây ra …)

  • diseases: cancer (ung thư), obesity (béo phì), being overweight, heart disease, eye strain (khô mắt), headache

“There are some ways to stay healthy. First, we should eat healthy food such as fish, meat, or vegetables because they can provide nutrition and vitamins which are good for our health. Second, it is better for us to play sports. It is fun and good for our health. I can relax after a long day at work and improve our health. Besides, I can keep fit and prevent health problems such as obesity.”

“Fast food is harmful to our health. It can cause some problems such as cancer, obesity, or heart disease. It is the leading cause of obesity. Therefore, we should not eat fast food.”

3. Chi phí (Cost / money)

Tính từ: cheap, affordable (kinh tế, k đắt), economical (kinh tế), expensive, suitable for

Cụm từ hữu ích:

  • reduce transportation costs and daily expenses (giảm chi phí đi lại và chi phí hàng ngày)

  • save money for other important things

  • waste money on … : lãng phí tiền vào …

  • spend (money) on … : chi tiền vào …

  • people with low income (thu nhập thấp)

  • people with limited budgets (ngân quỹ hạn hẹp)

“Travelling by bus is affordable. It helps people save a lot of money for other important things such as healthcare and education. It is suitable for students or people with limited budgets.”

4. Kiến thức (Knowledge)

Tính từ: useful

Cụm từ hữu ích:

  • improve (my) knowledge

  • broaden (my) mind

  • update information about the world

  • provide good/valuable lessons about …

Áp dụng cho chủ đề: travelling, watching TV, reading newspapers, clubs, courses

“Travelling is useful. It helps people broaden their mind about the world.”

“The club is really useful for members. It provides good lessons about gardening.”

“Watching TV is useful. It helps people broaden their mind and update information about the world. Besides, it provides good lessons about science, technology or languages.”

“We can offer a free cooking course. It is really useful for members. It provides good lessons about cooking, nutrition or healthcare.”

5. Kỹ năng (Skills)

Tính từ: useful

Cụm từ hữu ích:

  • develop/improve many skills

  • communication skills: kn giao tiếp)

  • leadership skills: kn lãnh đạo)

  • language skills

  • public speaking skill: kĩ năng nói trước đám đông

  • organizational skill: kn tổ chức)

  • manage time: quản lý tg)

  • make friends with many people (from different countries)

  • become more independent/confident (độc lập, tự tin)

Áp dụng cho chủ đề: volunteering, relaxation activities, clubs, classes, courses, travelling, living abroad

“Volunteering is useful and meaningful for children. It helps them develop and improve many skills such as communication, leadership and organizational skills.”

“Living in another country is hard/challenging but really useful. It helps people improve many skills such as communication and language skills. Besides, it allows people to make friends with people from different countries and become more independent. People learn how to manage time and cook.”

6. Sự thuận tiện (Convenience)

Tính từ: convenient, suitable for busy people

Cụm từ hữu ích:

  • can (stay at home)

  • don't have to (go out)

  • can (go/learn/shop…) anywhere at any time

Áp dụng cho chủ đề: cars/motorbikes, online learning, online shopping, fast food, e-books

“I often travel by motorbike. It is really convenient in big cities. People can go everywhere.”

“Online learning is really convenient. It is suitable for busy people. They can stay at home and learn with their computer. They don't have to go to class. They can learn anywhere at any time.”

“Online shopping is really convenient for busy people. They can stay at home and order everything. They don't have to go to stores. They can buy things anywhere at any time.”

“Fast food is really convenient for busy people. They can buy it anywhere at any time. They don't have to go to the market, cook or clean up. Besides, it is affordable for students, workers or people with low income.”

7. Địa điểm (Place)

Khung miêu tả: tên địa điểm – ở đâu – như thế nào – yếu tố (không khí, con người, đồ ăn, chi phí) – hoạt động – cảm xúc

Tính từ miêu tả: exciting, busy, noisy, crowded, expensive, polluted, quiet, peaceful, beautiful, stunning, famous, well-known

Cụm từ theo yếu tố:

  • không khí: The air is fresh and pleasant.

  • con người: The people are friendly and kind (hospitable, honest, hard-working).

  • đồ ăn: The food is delicious, healthy and cheap.

  • chi phí sinh hoạt: The living costs are low/affordable.

Hoạt động:

  • visit famous places (tourist attractions/travel destinations/places of interest)

  • climb the mountains

  • swim in the sea

  • eat/enjoy seafood

  • enjoy fresh air:

  • go sightseeing: đi ngắm cảnh

  • take photos

Áp dụng cho chủ đề: hometown, favourite place, travel destination, place of interest

“My favourite place is Halong Bay, in Quang Ninh, not far from Hanoi (about 100km). It is stunning and famous. It is one of the most popular travel destinations in Vietnam. Here we can swim in the sea, eat seafood and climb the mountains. The air is fresh. The people are friendly and kind. And the food is delicious and cheap. I really like Halong.”

“I am going to talk about my favourite place, Dalat — one of the most popular travel destinations in Vietnam, not very far from Ho Chi Minh City (about 200km). It is beautiful, with many kinds of flowers, and the weather is cool all year round. The food is delicious, healthy and cheap. The people are friendly and kind (hospitable and kind-hearted); they always help other people. Here we can visit flower gardens, climb the hills, walk around Xuan Huong Lake, take nice photos and go sightseeing. It helps people relax after long days at work and share happy moments with family and friends. I really love Dalat.”

Các dạng địa điểm khác

Hỏi: Miền quê (countryside/rural area)

Đáp: I like to live in the countryside because it is quiet and peaceful. The air is fresh and good for my health. The people are friendly and kind — they always help other people. The food is delicious, healthy and cheap, so I can save money. However, it is a little boring.

Hỏi: Thành phố (city)

Đáp: I like to live in a big city because it is exciting and convenient. Here I can get a good job and enjoy many kinds of entertainment. Besides, I can enjoy good healthcare and education. However, it is noisy and polluted, which is not good for my health.

Hỏi: Bãi biển (beach)

Đáp: I prefer spending time on the beach because it is really exciting. I love to swim in the sea and eat seafood. Besides, I like sunbathing and walking along the beach.

Hỏi: Núi (mountains)

Đáp: I like to spend time in the mountains because it is quiet and peaceful. The air is fresh and pleasant. I love to climb the mountains and explore nature, enjoying beautiful scenery with green trees and many kinds of flowers.

Hỏi: Trong nhà (indoors) — learn/work/relax

Đáp: I like to learn/work/relax indoors because it is quiet and peaceful, so I can focus on my work/study. Besides, I feel more comfortable. I don't like working/studying outdoors because it depends on the weather — if it's hot, I feel tired.

Hỏi: Ngoài trời (outdoors)

Đáp: I like to work/study/learn outdoors because I can enjoy fresh air. It is good for my health. Besides, I can meet people, so I can improve my communication skills.

Hỏi: Văn phòng và nhà máy (office vs. factory/workshop)

Đáp: I like to work in an office because it is quiet, so I can focus on my work, and I feel more comfortable — it is good for my health. I don't like working in a factory (workshop) because it is noisy and polluted. Besides, the work is physically demanding — for example, people have to stand for long hours. It is not good for our health. (An alternative view: some people like factory work because it is exciting, and they can meet and talk to many people.)

8. Con người (People)

Khung miêu tả: thông tin cá nhân (tên, tuổi, quê quán, nghề nghiệp) – ngoại hình – tính cách – sở thích

Ngoại hình: beautiful, handsome, good-looking, long hair, black eyes

Tính cách: friendly, kind, kind-hearted, honest, humorous

Áp dụng cho chủ đề: best friend, favourite person, singer, film star

“My best friend is Mary. She is 40 years old. She is from Vietnam. She is a doctor at a big hospital in Hanoi. She is beautiful, with long hair and black eyes. She is friendly and kind — she always helps other people, poor and disabled people.”

“My favourite singer/film star is My Tam. She is a famous singer in Vietnam with a strong and sweet voice. She is pretty, with blonde hair and smiling eyes. She is friendly and kind-hearted — she always helps other people, especially poor or disabled people. I have heard so many touching stories about her. I really like and admire her.”

9. Sở thích & thời gian rảnh (Hobbies & free time)

“In my free time, I like playing sports. I often play football with my friends at the weekend. It is fun and useful — it helps me relax after a long day at work and share happy moments with my friends. Also, it allows me to stay healthy and keep fit, so I can prevent many health problems. Sometimes I go to a café near my house, where I can drink coffee, enjoy music and chat with friends. It's relaxing.”

Câu hỏi thường gặp: What do you often do in your free time? · What's your hobby? · What did you do yesterday? · What did you do on your last holiday?

10. Thực phẩm (Food)

Đồ ăn lành mạnh (healthy food): meat, fish, eggs, seafood, vegetables, fruits, salad, soup

  • good for our health

  • provide nutrition and vitamins which are important/necessary for our body (cung cấp dưỡng chất và vitamin cần thiết cho cơ thể)

  • help people stay healthy and prevent health problems (ngăn ngừa bệnh)

Đồ ăn nhanh (fast food)

  • fast, convenient, affordable, suitable for busy people/people with limited budgets

  • save time

  • don’t have to cook or clean up

  • not good for our health

  • can cause health problems such as cancer, obesity, heart disease

Ăn ở nhà / ăn ngoài

  • eat at home: home-made dishes, healthy, affordable/economical, save money, share happy moments with family

  • eat out/eat in a restaurant: many kinds of food, delicious

Câu hỏi & câu trả lời mẫu

Hỏi: What do you eat for breakfast?

Đáp: I often eat bread because it is fast and convenient — I can save time for other things. Sometimes I eat Pho because it is delicious and healthy.

Hỏi: What is a typical meal in your country?

Đáp: In my country we have 3 meals a day. For breakfast we eat Banh Mi, Pho or rice. For lunch and dinner we eat meat, fish or eggs with vegetables and fruits.

Hỏi: What kind of food do you like? Which do you prefer, healthy food or fast food?

Đáp: I like healthy food such as meat, fish, eggs, vegetables or fruits because it is good for my health — it provides nutrition and vitamins that are important for my body, and it helps me stay healthy and prevent health problems. (Alternative: I like fast food because it is fast and convenient — I can save time and don't have to cook or clean up. It is suitable for me because I am busy, and it is affordable, so I can save money.)

Hỏi: What are the differences/attractions of healthy food and fast food?

Đáp: Both kinds of food have different attractions, depending on people's interests and budgets. Some people like fast food because it is fast and convenient; for me, I prefer healthy food because it is good for my health.

Hỏi: Should fast food be imposed high taxes?

Đáp: In my opinion, fast food should be taxed highly because it is not good for our health — it can cause health problems such as cancer and obesity.

Câu hỏi khác chưa trả lời chi tiết: Where do you like to eat, at home or in a restaurant? · Do you often eat out?

Đáp: I prefer eating at home because it is affordable and healthy — I can save money and share happy moments with my family. I don't like eating in a restaurant because it is expensive and I don't like going out.

11. Giao thông (Transport)

Từ vựng:

  • plane/airplane, airport, flight (chuyến bay), wait for a flight

  • car / motorbike / bicycle / bus / train

  • train/railway station (ga tàu), bus stop

  • trip/journey (chuyến đi)

Lợi ích chung: fast, safe, convenient (go anywhere at any time), comfortable

Phương tiện công cộng (public transport): affordable (kinh tế), suitable for students/workers/people with low income; good for the environment — reduce air pollution (giảm sự ô nhiễm không khí), reduce traffic jams (giảm tắc nghẽn giao thông)

Câu hỏi & câu trả lời mẫu

Hỏi: How do you travel around? / How did you come here?

Đáp: I often travel by motorbike because it is fast and convenient — I can save time and go anywhere at any time. Besides, it is affordable, so I can save money. (Alternative: I often travel by bus because it is affordable, so I can save money, and it is good for the environment — it can reduce air pollution and traffic jams.)

Đáp: I came here by car. I left home at 5am and arrived at 7am — the traffic today was heavy, and the streets were crowded with people and cars.

Hỏi: The government should encourage people to use public transport. What are the benefits?

Đáp: There are some benefits of public transport. First, it is affordable and suitable for people with low income. Second, it is good for the environment — it can reduce air pollution and traffic jams.

Câu hỏi khác: What is the most popular means of transport in your city? · Do you often travel by public transport? · Which do you prefer, travelling by air or by train? · Describe the picture (a man standing at an airport).

12. Mua sắm (Shopping)

Từ vựng:

  • shop (n, v)

  • go shopping

  • do some shopping (đi chợ, mua đồ thiết yếu)

  • buy (bought)

  • goods/product/item/thing (hàng hoá/sản phẩm)

Chợ / chợ dân sinh (market)

affordable — save money; fresh

Siêu thị / trung tâm mua sắm (supermarket / mall)

many kinds of products; high quality; comfortable; clean

Mua sắm trực tuyến (online shopping)

convenient; don't have to go out — save time; buy from anywhere; compare prices (so sánh giá cả)

Cửa hàng trực tiếp (physical store)

check the quality of the products; try on; price (cheap, expensive, reasonable)

Câu hỏi & câu trả lời mẫu

Hỏi: When was the last time you went shopping?

Đáp: Last week I went shopping with my friends at the supermarket near my house. It was fun — it helped me relax after work and reduce stress, and I could share happy moments with my friends. I bought a shirt for 200 dollars; it was beautiful and I really liked it.

Hỏi: Where do you often buy food?

Đáp: I often buy food at the local market near my house. It is fast and convenient, so I can save time, and the food there is delicious and fresh.

Hỏi: Do you think online shopping will be popular in the future?

Đáp: I think online shopping will become popular in the future because it is very convenient — people don't have to go out, they can stay home and order things. Besides, people can compare prices easily, so they can buy things at cheaper prices.

Câu hỏi khác: Where do you prefer to buy things — local market or shops/supermarket? · How often do you go shopping?

Phần II — Miêu tả cảm xúc (Feelings)

1. Tính từ cảm xúc: đuôi -ed và -ing

Đuôi -ed miêu tả cảm giác của người; đuôi -ing miêu tả tính chất của sự vật/sự việc gây ra cảm giác đó.

-ed (cảm giác của người) -ing (tính chất gây ra cảm giác)
Tired Tiring
Excited Exciting
Surprised Surprising
Disappointed Disappointing
Relaxed Relaxing
Stressed Stressful
Bored Boring
Interested Interesting

Ví dụ

  • I felt tired because the exam was tiring.

  • The story is interesting. I am interested in the story.

  • He is bored. / He is boring. (hai nghĩa khác nhau)

  • I feel relaxed. Listening to music is relaxing.

  • I was tired and stressed. The exam was tiring and stressful.

2. Cấu trúc câu miêu tả cảm xúc

Cấu trúc cơ bản: S + be/feel + adj — S + was/were/felt + adj

Eg: I am happy. / I feel happy. / I was happy. / I felt happy.

Thêm mức độ: quite / a little / so / very + adj

Eg: I was a little disappointed.

3. Kể chuyện theo diễn biến cảm xúc

On that day, I had many different feelings.

  • At first I felt … but then I felt ….. When … finished, I felt ….

  • Before the (exam/trip/plan …), I felt ….. But then I felt …..

“At first, I felt nervous but when I got the result, I felt happy and proud of myself.”

“At first, I felt a little nervous but then I felt better. Although this happened a long time ago, everything is still in my mind and I will never forget.”

“I felt so excited when I heard the news, and I believe that most of the members share my feelings.”

“I felt so excited when I heard the news because I had been waiting for the event for a long time.”

4. Bảng cảm xúc & lý do thường dùng

Cảm xúc Cấu trúc kể chuyện Lý do thường dùng
Tích cực: happy, excited (phấn khích), delighted (vui), relaxed (thư giãn), be on cloud nine (9 tầng mây), proud of (tự hào), relieved (nhẹ nhõm), thrilled (sung sướng), surprised (ngạc nhiên)

-I was upset/happy/surprised when I heard that news.

- On that day, I had many different feelings/emotions.

- At first, I felt …. But then I felt …..

- Before the trip (exam, presentation, concert, meeting…) I felt….

- But after that I felt …. because …

- When I finished ….. I felt …. because …

- At the end of the …., I felt ….. (Eg: - - At the end of the trip, I felt so relaxed and comfortable.)

- Later on (sau này), I felt confident ….

- Although this happened a long time ago, everything is still in my mind and I will never forget.

  • Because I had never been there (played an extreme sport).

  • Because I had never been in this situation before.

  • Because it was beyond my expectation.

  • Because I hadn't seen her for a long time.

  • Because I had been waiting for a long time.

  • Because I had done a good thing.

  • Because it was my first time (I went to Dalat) = I had never been to Dalat before.

  • Because there were so many people.

  • Because the landscape was so beautiful.
Tiêu cực: feel down (buồn), sad, upset (buồn), worried (lo lắng), nervous (hồi hộp, lo lắng), scared (sợ), disappointed (thất vọng), tired (mệt), stressed (căng thẳng), depressed (chán) (dùng chung các cấu trúc kể chuyện ở trên) (dùng chung các lý do ở trên, đổi cho phù hợp ngữ cảnh)

Đoạn văn mẫu kể chuyện cảm xúc

“Before the trip I was very excited because I had never been there. When I arrived, wow, Dalat was so stunning with hills, trees, and many kinds of flowers. When I came home, I felt so relaxed and really wanted to return to Dalat one day. Although this happened a long time ago, everything is still in my mind and I will never forget.”

“At first, I felt really nervous because I had never spoken in front of many people. But after a few minutes, I felt better and tried to speak confidently. When I finished my talk, everyone stood up and applauded. I felt so happy and proud of myself because I had tried my best. Although this happened a long time ago, everything is still in my mind and I will never forget.”

“Before the exam, I felt so tired and stressed because the exam was so difficult (challenging). But when I got the result, I felt relieved — it's amazing. I was so happy and proud of myself because I had tried my best. Although this happened a long time ago, everything is still in my mind and I will never forget.”

“When I got the gift (present), I felt so excited that I jumped up and down like a child. It was one of the best presents I had ever got. I was grateful — the gift was so meaningful.”

“I felt so relaxed and comfortable after busy days. It was one of the most memorable experiences in my life.”

5. Miêu tả người / vật / hoạt động (tính từ chung)

Cấu trúc: S + be + adj

  • She is beautiful and intelligent.

  • The books are interesting and useful.

  • Listening to music is enjoyable and relaxing.

  • Playing games is fun.

Phần III — Ngân hàng câu hỏi luyện nói bổ sung

Các câu hỏi luyện tập còn lại (đã loại bỏ trùng lặp với Phần I), gộp theo nhóm chủ đề.

Thể thao & sức khỏe

Câu hỏi Câu trả lời mẫu
What kind of sport do you like to play? I like to play football with my friends at the weekend because it is fun and good for my health. I can relax after a busy day and share happy moments with my friends. Besides, I can stay healthy and keep fit.
Do you like outdoor sports or indoor sports? I like outdoor sports because they are more interesting. I can enjoy fresh air, which is good for my health. Besides, I can meet many people, so I can improve my communication skills.
Which do you prefer to play, chess or video games? I prefer to play chess because it is interesting — I can relax after work and reduce stress. I don't like playing video games because they are not good for my health; they can cause problems such as eye strain and headaches.
Should children play video games? In my opinion, children should not play video games because it is not good for their health — it can cause problems such as eye strain and headaches. Besides, they don't have time for other activities, especially physical activities.

Mua sắm & giao thông

Câu hỏi Câu trả lời mẫu
How often do you go shopping? I go shopping once a week, with my friends, at the weekend. It's fun — I can relax after a busy day and share happy moments with my friends.
How do you often travel in your city? I often travel by bus. It is affordable and good for the environment — it can reduce air pollution and traffic jams. It is suitable for people with low income.

Công nghệ & quan điểm

Câu hỏi Câu trả lời mẫu
Online learning is becoming more and more popular. What are the benefits? There are some benefits of online learning. First, it is convenient — I can study anywhere at any time, which is suitable for working and busy people. Besides, it is affordable, so people can save money for other things.
Is volunteering important for children? / Why do people enjoy reading books? / What are the benefits of indoor activities? (Trả lời theo mẫu ở mục Kiến thức / Kỹ năng / Địa điểm — Phần I, tuỳ chủ đề cụ thể.)

Câu hỏi Câu trả lời mẫu
Tell me about your friends. / What do you often do with your friends? I have many friends. They are friendly and kind — they always help me when I need it. In our free time, we often play sports together. It's fun.
What's your favourite place? / Where do you often go to relax? My favourite place is Halong Bay. It is beautiful and famous — one of the most popular travel destinations in Vietnam. The weather is very nice, sunny and perfect for outdoor activities. The people are friendly and kind, and the food is delicious and healthy. I really like it.
Where do you prefer to live? A small community or a big city? I prefer to live in a small community because it is quiet and peaceful. The air is fresh, the people are friendly and kind, and the food is delicious and healthy. I don't like living in a big city because it is noisy and polluted, which is not good for my health.
Where do you like to work, indoors or outdoors? I like to work outdoors because I can enjoy fresh air, which is good for my health. Besides, I can meet people, so I can improve my communication skills. I don't like working indoors because I find it a little boring.
Why do you learn English? I learn English because it is useful. It helps me get a good job and communicate with people all over the world. Besides, I can understand materials or movies in English.
