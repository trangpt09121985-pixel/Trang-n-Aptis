Writing – Social Club (bài giảng) (đáp án mới)

Social Club (50’)

Part 1: You want to join a social club. Fill in the form. Use 1 – 5 words. (5p) (luyện Chương 2 – Writing – Hướng dẫn trả lời Part 1 – hơn 8’)

  1. What’s the weather like today? – Sunny/It’s sunny./ It’s sunny and warm.

  2. How are you? – Fine/ I’m fine/ I am hungry.

  3. What’s your favorite hobby? – I love playing sports/ I’m into playing sports.

  4. How do you get to work? – By car/ I drive to work.

  5. What do you like to do in the evening? – I like to watch TV.

Part 2: You are a new member of a social club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20 – 30 words. (10p)

Tell me about your best friend (name, age, from, married, family, job, where, hobby, appearance, personality)

  • My best friend is Mary. She is small and pretty. She is friendly and kind. She always helps me when I need.

  • My best friend is Mary, who lives in the same building with me. We have been friends for over 10 years. She is sociable and reliable.

  • Why did you decide to join the club?

  • I decided to join the club because I love gardening and I want to meet people who have the same hobby. (Gardening club)

  • I decided to join the club because I’m into gardening and I would like to meet people who share the same hobby.

Like/love/enjoy/ be interested in/ be keen on/ be into/ find a lot of interest in/ big fan of…

Part 3: You are a member of a social club. You are talking to three other members in the social club chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30 – 40 words. (15p)

A: What do you do when you go out with your friends?

  • We often go to a café near my house. Here we can drink coffee, listen to music and talk about work and study. It’s fun and relaxing. It helps me reduce stress after work.

  • We often go to a café which is located in the city centre. Here we can drink the best coffee, enjoy our favorite music, and chat about work and study. It allows me to reduce stress after a hard-working day

B: Is it easier for young people to have friends?

  • Yes. It is easier for young people to have friends because they join many clubs and they can meet many people. They use Facebook to connect people.

  • I believe that it is not difficult for young people to have friends because they join many clubs where they can make friends with many people. Also, they are active and sociable.

C: It is much easier to make new friends these days because of computers and mobile phones. Do you agree? Why or why not?

  • I strongly agree with the idea that it is much easier to make new friends these days because of computers and mobile phones because people can use social networking sites such as Facebook to connect other people

  • I totally agree with the idea because computers and mobiles allow people to connect other people fast and easily. They can use social networking sites such as Facebook to make friends with many people. It’s really convenient.

Câu đệm tăng số từ:

  • Hi. It’s nice to talk to you. My name is Mary

  • Hi. I like your question. It’s really interesting.

  • To be honest, I have no ideas about this. I think I need more time to think about the question.

  • Develop ideas: what, who, when, why, where, how often…

Egg: What do you do in your free time?

I play sports. I often play football with my friends at the weekend. It is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after work and improve my health. We play in a park near my house.

  • Why…? – Because it is + adj. It helps/allows/enables …

Egg: Why do you like reading books? – I like reading books because it is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after work and broaden my mind. (I often read books in the library at the weekend)

  • Topics: place, person, hobby

  • Place: I was born in Halong Bay. It is in Quang Ninh. It’s not very far from Hanoi, about 100km. It’s beautiful with islands, bays, mountains, and so on. It’s the most popular travel destination in Vietnam. Here we can swim in the sea, eat seafood and climb the mountains.

  • Person: My favorite singer is My Tam. She is from Quang Nam. She is the most famous singer in Vietnam. Her voice is strong and sweet. She is friendly and kind. She always helps other people, especially poor people. I really like her.

  • Hobby: I love playing sports. I often play football with my friends at the weekend. It’s enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after work and improve my health. We play in the park near my house.


Part 4: You are a member of a social club. You received this email from the club. (20p)

Dear members,

We would like to organize some monthly face-to-face meetings. We would like your suggestions about when and where we could meet. It suits both young and older people. Please send us your suggestions and your reasons.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

Wow, I just heard that our club is planning to organize some monthly face-to-face meetings. I'm so excited — especially because I'll get to meet other club members.

I think it would be better if we meet at the club on Sundays. What do you think? I'm planning to write to the manager and share my ideas.

See you soon!

  • (chào): Hi Jane,

  • (xưng danh): It’s me, Mary.

  • (hỏi thăm sk): How are you?/ How have you been?/ How’s it going?/ How are things?

  • (có nhận dc email?): Did you get the latest email from our club?

  • (tôi nghe nói rằng): I’ve heard that our club is going to organize some monthly face-to-face meetings.

  • (đưa ra dề xuâts): I think we could meet at the club on Sundays

  • (hỏi ý kiến banj): What do you think?/ What’s your take on it?

  • (cùng bạn bàn bạc): Let’s talk about it over lunch/coffee

  • (tam biệt): Bye/ See you soon
