Writing P4

(khung chung cho các dạng bài và 10 giải pháp dễ áp dụng nhất)

  1. Viết cho bạn

Hi Jane,

How’s it going? I just heard that (thông tin trong bài). I think we should ………… What do you think? Let’s talk about it over lunch.

See you later.

Mary.

VD:

Hi Jane,

I just heard that we are going to organize a sports event. I think we should organize in summer when students have their vacation. It is better for us to organize in Thonng Nhat Park because it is big and convenient. What do you think? Let’s talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Viết cho quản lý/President

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I have been an active member of the club for the last 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions/ideas about the upcoming (sắp tới) (event/meeting/plan/exhibition - triển lãm..) / problem mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that (………….). To be honest, I was truly delighted (sad/worried/disappointed) to hear that news.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, (idea 1 - giải thích). Second, (idea 2 - giải thích).

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards

VD:

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming event mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club is going to plan to encourage young people to cook at home. To be honest, I was so happy to hear that news.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should have a talk show and invite a master chef to come and talk about the benefits of cooking at home. It is practical and feasible to carry out. Second, we can offer a free cooking course for people who want to learn how to cook. It is the most effective in the long term.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards

Các giải pháp chống cháy – kèm giải thích ngắn gọn, dễ

  1. We should have a face-to-face or an online meeting for members to express their ideas directly. It is fast and easy to carry out (thực hiện)

  2. We should ask for help (xin sự trợ giúp) from an expert (chuyên gia) who has experience to deal with (giải quyết) this. It is the best way

  3. We should have a talk show/workshop (buổi nói chuyện/buổi tập huấn) and invite a famous person to come and talk about the benefits of (playing sports/reading books …). It is practical (thực tế) and feasible (khả thi) to carry out (thực hiện).

  4. We should educate people about the importance of (art/environment..). It is the most effective in the long term (hiệu qủa nhất về lâu dài)

  5. We should ask for help from the local government and organizations. It is fast and practical to carry out.

  6. We should work with the local government and local schools to introduce about the …

  7. We should promote the event (quảng bá) on social networking sites such as/including Facebook because it can reach more people. (tiếp cận)

(It is advisable for us to promote the event on social networking sites, especially Facebook because …)

  1. We should offer free (tickets/courses/training..) for (local people). It is practical and feasible/easy to carry out.

  2. We should not change anything because it can cause a lot of inconvenience (gây ra nhiều sự bất tiện) for everybody and it may discourage members. (làm nản các thành viên)

  3. We should set clear rules (đề ra quy định rõ ràng) about these (behaviors) (hành vi). It is the most effective in the long term.

  • We should /can (have a talk show)

  • It is better for us to (have a talk show)

  • It is advisable for us to…

  • I am thinking about (having a talk show/a talk show)

  • I suggest + V-ing (I suggest having a talk show)

  • I suggest we should (have a talk show)
