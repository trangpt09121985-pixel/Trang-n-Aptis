Writing – Walking Club – Europe (đáp án mới)

WALKING CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a walking club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your name? – My surname is Nguyen./ Nguyen.

  2. Where do you live? – I live in Vietnam./ Vietnam.

  3. How many people are there in your family? – There are four people./ Four people.

  4. What is your hobby? – I like watching movies./ Watching movies.

  5. What is your favorite singer? – It is Justin Bieber./ Justin Bieber.

Part 2: You are a new member of a walking club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Where do you often walk?

  • I often walk in the park near my house. Here I can enjoy fresh air and do some exercise. It's relaxing and beneficial for my health.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Tell me about the last time you walked.

  • The last time I walked was yesterday morning. I got up early and went to the park with my children. We walked for about 30 minutes before I went home and started my work.

B: Walking is good. What are useful ways to encourage people to walk more?

  • There are some ways to encourage people to walk more. First, we can educate people about the health benefits of walking. Second, we can organize walking events with attractive prizes.

C: Do you prefer slow walking or fast walking?

  • I prefer slow walking because it is more comfortable. Also, I can watch the trees and flowers along the road, listen to the birds sing and talk with other people.

Part 4: You are a member of the walking club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

The club has planned to organize a walk across European countries. A few members of the group suggested it, but others said it was unrealistic. Please send us your suggestions and your reasons.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club is planning to organize a walk across European countries. I think we should tell the manager that this plan is unrealistic. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming plan mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that the club is planning to organize a walk across European countries. To be honest, I was quite worried to hear that news, because the plan seems very unrealistic and risky for most members.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should have a meeting for members to express their ideas directly about a safer, shorter route instead, since it is fast and easy to carry out. Second, we should set clear rules about distance and safety, as this is the most effective solution in the long term.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
