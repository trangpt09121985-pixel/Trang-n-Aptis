Writing – Nature Club (đáp án mới)

NATURE CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a nature club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What language do you speak? – I speak Vietnamese./ Vietnamese.

  2. What's your favourite animal? – I love cats./ Cats.

  3. What do you like doing in the evening? – I enjoy watching TV./ Watching TV.

  4. How often do you go shopping? – Twice a week./ I go shopping twice a week.

  5. When was the last time you went out? – It was last Sunday./ Last Sunday.

Part 2: You are a new member of the Nature club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Please tell me about your free time and hobbies.

  • I enjoy playing sports. I often play soccer with my friends at the weekend. It's fun and helpful, because it helps me relax, improve my health and keep fit.

Part 3: You are a member of a nature club. You are talking to three other members of the club in the chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Hi! Welcome to the club. Where do you like to visit?

  • I like to visit Ha Long Bay. It's the most popular travel destination in Vietnam, with beautiful islands, mountains and caves. There, we can do interesting things like swimming in the sea, climbing the mountains and visiting caves.

B: Hi! Why did you decide to join the club?

  • I decided to take part in the club because I love the beauty of nature, and I would like to meet people who share the same hobby. I think we can share a lot of experiences together.

C: The bigger the club, the more environmentally conscious people are. Do you agree?

  • It is true that the bigger the club, the more environmentally conscious people become. Nowadays, more and more people are interested in the environment, because it directly affects our lives.

Part 4: You are a member of the nature club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

The club is planning to encourage young people to spend more time outdoors instead of staying at home and playing games.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club is planning to encourage young people to spend more time outdoors instead of staying home playing games. I think we should organise fun activities like camping or sports days. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming plan mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club is planning to encourage young people to spend more time outdoors instead of staying at home playing games. To be honest, I was very happy to hear that news, because it is good for their health.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should organise interesting outdoor activities such as camping, hiking or sports events, since these are fun and keep young people active. Second, we should educate people about the importance of spending time outdoors, as this is the most effective solution in the long term.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
