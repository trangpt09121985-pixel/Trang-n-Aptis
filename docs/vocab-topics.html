Vocab theo chủ đề

Phần I — Từ vựng theo chủ đề

  1. Tinh thần (Mental well-being) (mental health)

  • Fun, enjoyable, interesting, relaxing, amazing

  • Relax after work

  • Relax after a busy/long day

  • Reduce stress

  • Share happy moments with my family and friends

  • Forget all the troubles

VD1: What do you do in your free time?

  • In my free time, I often play games with my friends. It is fun. I can relax after work and share happy moments with my friends. Sometimes I play sports in the park near my house. It’s relaxing.

VD2: What kind of music do you like?

  • I like pop music. I often listen to music with my friends at the weekend. It is enjoyable. I can relax after work and forget all the troubles.

  1. Sức khỏe thể chất (Physical health)

  • Healthy, good for my health, strong, active, harmful to

  • Healthy food: meat, fish, eggs, vegetables, fruits..

  • Provide nutrition and vitamins which are important for our body

  • Improve my health

  • Stay active

  • Keep fit

  • Prevent health problems

  • Not good for our health

  • Cause some health problems such as cancer, obesity, heart disease, eye strain, headache

  • Fast food is the leading cause of obesity.

VD1: What’s your hobby?

  • I love playing sports. I often play sports with my friends at the weekend. It is fun and good for my health. I can relax after work and share happy moments with my friends. Besides, I can improve my health, keep fit and prevent health problems.

VD2: Which do you prefer? (healthy food – fast food)

  • I prefer healthy food because it is good for my health. It provides nutrition and vitamins which are important for our body. I don’t like fast food because it is harmful to our health. It can cause some health problems such as cancer and obesity. It is the leading cause of obesity.

  1. Chi phí (Cost / money)

  • Cheap, expensive, affordable, economical, reasonable, suitable for

  • People with low income

  • People with limited budgets

  • Save money (for other things)

  • Save money for other important things such as healthcare, education and so on

  • Money (= transportation costs, living costs)

  • Reduce living costs

VD: How do you travel around?

  • I travel by bus because it is affordable and suitable for people with low income. I can save money for other things.

VD2: Fast food is becoming more and more popular. What do you think?

  • I agree with the idea. Fast food is becoming more and more popular because it is fast and convenient. Besides, it is affordable and suitable for people with low income. People can save money for other things. (However, it is not good for our health. It can cause some health problems. It is the leading cause of obesity.)

  1. Kiến thức (Knowledge)

  • Useful

  • Update information about the world

  • Broaden (my) mind

  • Learn a lot of things

VD: How often do you watch TV?

  • I watch TV every day. It is interesting and useful. I can relax after work and share happy moments with my family. Besides, I can update information and broaden my mind.

  1. Kỹ năng (Skills)

  • Useful

  • Improve my communication skill

  • Learn many skills

  • Learn how to cook

  • Skills: communication skill, language skills (listening skill, speaking skill…), leadership, organizational skills

  • Important/necessary for our jobs/careers/future/everyone

  • Become more confident and independent

VD: Young people should learn how to cook because it is useful. They can become independent.

VD: Children should take part in social/outdoor activities because it is really useful. They can relax after work and learn many things. Besides, they can improve their communication and leadership skills which are important for their future jobs.

  1. Sự thuận tiện (Convenience) (fast food, online learning, online shopping, ..)

  • Convenient

  • Suitable for busy people

  • (learn/eat/shop..) anywhere at any time

  • Don’t have to (go out/cook/clean up…)

  • Save time for other things

VD: Do you learn online? – Yes, I do. It is convenient. I can learn anywhere at any time. I don’t have to go out so I can save a lot of time. It is suitable for busy people.

VD: What do you use your computer for? – I use my computer to learn online. It is convenient. I can learn anywhere at any time. I don’t have to go out so I can save a lot of time. Besides, I use my computer to play games. It is fun. I can relax after work and share happy moments with my friends

VD: Which kind of food do you like? (healthy – fast food)

  • I like fast food because it is convenient. I can eat anywhere at any time. I don’t have to cook or clean up.

VD: How do you travel around?

  • I often travel by motorbike because it is fast and convenient. I can go anywhere at any time. I can save time for other things

  1. Địa điểm (Place) (tên, ở đâu, như thế nào, hoạt động, thời tiết, con người, đồ ăn, cảm xúc)

  • I am going to talk about (my hometown/a famous place in my country..). It is Halong Bay, in Quang Ninh. It is beautiful and famous. It is the most popular place in Vietnam. Here I can swim in the sea, eat seafood and climb the mountain. The weather is very nice. It is sunny and perfect for outdoor activities. The people are friendly and kind. The food is delicious and healthy. I really like my hometown.

  1. Con người (People) (name, age, job, from, family, ngoại hình, tính cách, hoạt động, cảm xúc)

  • I am going to talk about my best friend. She is My Tam. She is 40. She is from HN. She is a teacher. She is beautiful with long hair and black eyes. She is friendly and kind. She always helps me when I need. In free time, we often go out together. I really like her
