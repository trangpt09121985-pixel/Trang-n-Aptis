Writing – Art Club (đáp án mới)

ART CLUB

Question 1: You want to join an Art club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What do you like doing with your friend? – Watching movies./ I like watching movies.

  2. Which sport do you like to play the most? – Football./ I like playing football.

  3. What did you do last night? – I stayed at home.

  4. Do you like shopping? – Yes, I do./ Yes, I like shopping.

  5. What is your job? – I'm a teacher./ A teacher.

Question 2: You are a new member of an Art club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words.

Tell me about a painting or photo you like.

  • I really like a photo of a beach at sunset. I took it during a trip with my family last summer, and the colors of the sky were beautiful.

Question 3: You are a member of the art club. You are talking to three other members in the art club chat room. Use 30-40 words.

A: I kept a painting for a long time. Tell me a thing that you have had for a long time.

  • I still have a small drawing that my grandmother gave me when I was a child. It is not very good, but I have kept it for more than 15 years because it means a lot to me.

B: I would like to learn painting, but I have not found an effective way. Should I take a course at my local college? Please, give me some advice.

  • Yes, I think taking a course would be a great idea. A teacher can give you useful feedback and correct your mistakes, which is much faster than learning completely by yourself.

C: Street art, where people paint on buildings, is becoming popular. However, some people criticize that it is bad. What is your opinion?

  • I don't think street art is bad. In my opinion, it makes cities more colorful and interesting, as long as artists paint in places where it is allowed.

Question 4: You are a member of an art club. You received this email from the club.

Dear all members,

The Art Club is organising a talk to the public to attract more attention. We are going to invite an artist to give a talk to the members. As a member of our club, could you give us an artist to join our talk and what topic should they share to gain more attention? We would like to have more young and elderly members.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend - also a member of the club. (50 words)

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our art club wants to invite an artist to give a talk to attract more members. I think we should invite a street artist because it's popular with young people. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the organiser of the plan. (120-150 words)

Dear Organiser,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the art club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my ideas about the upcoming talk mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the club wants to invite an artist to talk to the members and the public. I was very excited to hear that news, because I think it is a great way to attract more people.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I suggest inviting a well-known street artist to talk about modern art, because this topic is interesting and popular with young people. Second, the artist could also give a short live painting demonstration, which would attract both young and elderly visitors.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
