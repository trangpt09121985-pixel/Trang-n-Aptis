Writing – Car Club (đáp án mới)

CAR CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a car club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Do you have a driving licence? – Yes, I do./ I have a driving licence.

  2. How do you usually travel to work or school? – By car./ I travel by car.

  3. What's your favourite car brand? – Toyota./ I love Toyota.

  4. How often do you travel by car? – Every day./ I travel by car every day.

  5. Who usually drives in your family? – My father./ My father usually drives.

Part 2: You are a new member of the Car club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Do you usually travel by car?

  • Yes, I usually travel by car. It's convenient and comfortable because I can go anywhere at any time. However, it is not affordable.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Tell me about a time you traveled by car.

  • Last year, I went to Da Lat with my family by car. It took about 5 hours to go there. On the way, we talked or listened to music. It was really fun because we could share happy moments together

B: Tell me about a memorable experience you had while traveling by car.

  • As far as I can remember, it was about 5 years ago when I was living in Hanoi. One day when I was on the way home, my car was broken. It was terrible because it was very hot outside.

C: Should we take public transportation or buy a car?

  • In my opinion, we should take public transportation because it is affordable and good for the environment. We can save money for other important things. Besides, it helps reduce air pollution and traffic jams.

Part 4: You are a member of the Car club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

There will be a car programme running across Europe. The programme invites the Car Club to send two representatives (one main driver and one passenger), and both must have good driving skills. Do you have any suggestions for these positions?

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club needs to choose two members to represent us in the car programme in Europe, one driver and one passenger. I think we should choose the most experienced and confident drivers. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming car programme mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club needs to choose two representatives, one main driver and one passenger, to take part in a car programme across Europe. To be honest, I was very excited to hear that news, as it is a great opportunity for our club.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should choose members who have strong driving skills and enough experience to handle long journeys safely. Second, it is advisable to hold a short interview to select the most suitable candidates fairly.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
