Part 4:

Dear members,

Our club has two plans: First, create a meeting to help members solve questions during the sales process. Second, organize a training session for members.

Please choose one of the above 2 plans and explain why.

  • Write an email to your friend

  • Write an email to the manager

Part 4: Dear member,

Currently our club is planning to support local individuals who want to start up a small business. The club has 2 proposals: First, create a call center where customers can call to ask questions and request help. Second, create free courses to support local universities nearby. Let us know which option you would use and why.

  1. Write a letter to a friend about your choice and the idea. (50 words)

  2. Write a letter to the club manager about your choice and why. (120 - 150 words)

  • Offer free courses to support local universities nearby.
    (I think the club should offer free courses because they can provide useful knowledge and skills for students who want to start a small business. This is practical and affordable to carry out because many people can join without worrying about the cost. Therefore, the club can support local individuals and build a stronger relationship with the community.)

  • Invite successful business owners to share their experience in the courses.
    (I think the club should invite famous or successful business owners to give talks because they have experience and expertise in this field. They can share useful advice about starting a business. Therefore, participants can learn practical skills and feel more confident about their future plans.)
