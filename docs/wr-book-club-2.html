Writing – Book Club 2 (đáp án mới)

BOOK CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a book club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your favorite color? – I like pink./ Pink.

  2. Where is your hometown? – My hometown is Yen Bai./ Yen Bai.

  3. What is your favorite season? – I love spring./ Spring.

  4. What is your favorite place? – I love my house./ My house.

  5. How old are you? – 19 years old./ I'm 19 years old.

Part 2: You are a new member of the book club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Why did you join the club?

  • I really like reading books. It is fun and useful. It helps me relax after work, reduce stress and broaden my mind, because there are many interesting stories and useful lessons.

Part 3: You are a new member of the book club. You are talking to other members in the chat room. Reply to their questions using sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: What is your favorite kind of book?

  • I am really interested in science books, which help me understand more about the world around us. I am curious about how things work, and science also allows us to have inventions that make our lives better.

B: When and where do you read books?

  • I usually read books after studying or at the weekend when I have free time. I often go to the library near my house because I can borrow many good books there, or sometimes go to a café instead.

C: Many people are too busy to read books. What do you think?

  • I agree that many people are too busy to read books. However, this is not good for their mental and physical health. They should make some time to relax, since reading is an easy way to reduce stress and feel better.

Part 4: You are a member of the book club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

The local authorities have announced that the public library may close next year because few people go there to read books. This may affect communities who like reading. What do you think about this? Do you have any other suggestions?

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that the public library might close next year because not enough people go there to read. I think we should offer free books or reading events to attract more visitors. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the possible closure of the public library mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that the local authorities may close the public library next year because too few people go there to read, which would affect the whole community. To be honest, I was very sad to hear this news.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should offer free books and reading events for local people, which is practical and easy to arrange and could attract new visitors. Second, we should promote the library and its activities on Facebook, since this can reach many more people, especially young readers.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
