Buổi 2 - Từ vựng theo chủ đề

Tinh thần

(mental health)

Adjs: Fun, enjoyable, interesting, relaxing

- relax after a long day at work and reduce stress

- share happy moments with family and friends

- forget all the worries and troubles

I like travelling. It is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after a long/busy/hard-working day at work and reduce stress. Besides, I can share happy moments with family and friends. Also, travelling allows me to forget all the worries and troubles

I can…./ It helps/allows me….

Sức khoẻ

(physical health)

Adjs: healthy, active (năng động), strong, harmful to (có hại cho), not good for

- eat healthy food

- drink enough water

- eat more vegetables and fruits

- improve (my) health

- stay active/healthy

- burn calories

- keep fit: giữ dáng

- prevent health problems: ngăn chặn các vấn đề về sk

- cause some health problems: gây ra

- diseases: cancer (ung thư), obesity (béo phì), being overweight, heart disease, eye strain (khô mắt), headache

Eg: There are some ways to stay healthy. First, we should eat healthy food such as fish, meat, or vegetables. Second, it is better for us to play sports. It is fun and good for our health. It helps me relax after a long day at work and improve our health. Besides, it allows us to keep fit and prevent health problems such as obesity.

Fast food/ playing video games

  • Fast food is harmful to our health. It can cause some problems such as cancer, obesity, or heart disease. It is the leading cause of obesity. Therefore, we should not eat fast food

Chi phí

(money)

Adjs: cheap, affordable (k đắt/k tốn nhiều), economical, expensive, suitable for

- Save money for other important things

- Waste money on: lãng phí tiền vào …

- Spend (money) on: chi tiền vào

- People with low income: những người có thu nhập thấp

- People with limited budgets: những người có ngân quỹ hạn hẹp

 Eg: Travelling by bus is affordable. It helps people save a lot of money for other important things such as healthcare and education. It is suitable for students or people with limited budgets.

Nơi chốn

(Place)

 I am going to talk about my favourite place. It is Dalat, one of the most popular travel destinations of Vietnam. It is not very far from HCM city, about 200km. It is beautiful with many kinds of flowers. The weather is very nice. It is cool all year round. The food is delicious, healthy and cheap. The people are friendly and kind (hospitable and kind-hearted). They always help other people. Here we can have many interesting activities. We can visit many flower gardens, climb the hills or walk around Xuan Huong lake. We can take many nice photos and go sightseeing. It’s amazing. It helps people relax after long days at work and share happy moments with families and friends. I really love Dalat

Người

(people)

 I am going to tell you about my favourite person. It is My Tam. She is a famous singer in Vietnam. She has a strong and sweet voice. I really like listening to her songs. She is pretty with blonde hair and smiling eyes. She is friendly and kind-hearted. She always helps other people, especially poor or disabled ones. I have heard so many touching stories about her. I really like and admire her

sở thích

(hobby)

In my free time, I like playing sports. I often play football with my friends at the weekend. It is fun and useful. It helps me relax after a long day at work and share happy moments with my friends. Also, it allows me to stay healthy and keep fit so I can prevent many health problems.

Sometimes I go to a café near my house. Here I can drink coffee, enjoy music and chat with friends. It’s relaxing and

  • Where do you like to eat? At home or in a restaurant?

  • Why do you learn English?
