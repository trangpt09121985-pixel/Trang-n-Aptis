Writing – Travel Club (đáp án mới)

TRAVEL CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a travel club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your favourite animal? – I love cats and dogs./ Cats and dogs.

  2. What time do you get up every day? – I get up at 6 am./ At 6 am.

  3. What language do you speak? – I speak English./ English.

  4. How many people are there in your family? – There are 4 people./ 4 people.

  5. Do you like to drink coffee? – I really like coffee./ Yes, I do.

Part 2: You are a new member of a travel club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Why are you interested in travel?

  • I am interested in travel because it is interesting and useful. It helps me relax and reduce stress. Besides, it allows me to broaden my mind and improve my knowledge.

  • I find a lot of interest in travel because travelling is enjoyable and beneficial. It helps me relax and reduce stress, and it improves my knowledge of the world.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Tell me about a time you had a trip in bad weather.

  • I had a trip in bad weather two years ago when I visited Ha Long Bay with my family. It started to rain heavily while we were on the boat, and we were scared, but the tour guide reassured us.

B: Travelling a long distance by some means of transportation is not good for the environment. Do you agree?

  • I totally agree with this statement. Some kinds of transportation for long distances, like airplanes, trains, ships or cars, can seriously pollute the air and water, which can directly affect people's health.

C: What are the most interesting places to visit in your country?

  • There are many popular tourist attractions in my country, including Ha Long Bay, Da Lat and Hanoi. There, visitors can enjoy the beauty of nature, try local food, explore new places and meet hospitable people.

Part 4: You are a member of the travel club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

The club is preparing to welcome a group of foreign tourists visiting our country for the first time. Do you have any suggestions to welcome them and communicate with them, since they do not speak Vietnamese?

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club is preparing to welcome a group of foreign tourists who don't speak Vietnamese. I think we should hire an interpreter to help us communicate with them. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the visit mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club is preparing to welcome a group of foreign tourists visiting our country for the first time, and they do not speak Vietnamese. To be honest, I was so happy to hear that news, because it is a great chance to practise my English and share our culture.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should ask for help from an expert, such as a professional interpreter, to help us communicate with them. It is the best way to avoid misunderstandings. Second, we should organize a welcome party so the tourists feel comfortable in our country.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
