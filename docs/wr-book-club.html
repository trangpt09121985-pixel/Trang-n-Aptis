Writing – Book Club (đáp án mới)

BOOK CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a Book club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Which sport is the most popular in your country? – Football./ It's football.

  2. What do you like doing with your friend? – Watching movies./ I like watching movies.

  3. Which sport do you like to play the most? – Football./ I like playing football.

  4. What did you do last night? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  5. What do you do on the weekend? – Playing football./ I play football with friends.

Part 2: You want to join a book club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words.

When and where do you usually read books?

  • I usually read books at home in my bedroom before going to sleep. On weekends, I sometimes read at a quiet coffee shop near my house instead.

Part 3: You are talking to other members of the club in the chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer.

Person A: Tell me about your favourite time and place to read a book?

  • My favorite time to read is late at night, when the house is quiet. I usually sit in my bedroom with a cup of tea and enjoy a good novel for an hour.

Person B: I bought a book as a gift for my friend but I don't know what kind of book he likes. Can you give me some advice?

  • You could ask your friend what kind of stories he likes, such as adventure or mystery. If you're not sure, a bestseller or an award-winning book is usually a safe choice.

Person C: In the future, nobody reads traditional books as everything is on the computer. What do you think about this?

  • I don't fully agree. Although many people now read e-books, I think traditional books will always have fans who enjoy the feeling of holding a real book in their hands.

  1. Write an email to your friend, who is also a member of the group. (50 words)

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How are you? I just heard that our club wants to invite a famous author to speak at an event. I think we should invite a well-known novelist. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion. (120-150 words)

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the book club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my ideas about the upcoming event mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, our club wants to invite a famous author to give a talk to the public. I was very excited to hear that news, because it is a wonderful opportunity for our members.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I suggest inviting a well-known novelist, because their stories are popular and interesting to a wide audience. Second, the author could talk about their writing journey and inspiration, which would be useful and motivating for members who enjoy writing.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
