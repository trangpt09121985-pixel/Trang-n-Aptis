Relative clauses (Mai Hien)

RELATIVE CLAUSES

(By Mai Hien)

  1. We should invite an artist who is famous/who is interested in this project.

It is advisable for us to invite a famous artist who is really interested in this project.

  1. I like the photo which was taken last year/ which is on the wall.

I really like the photo which was taken two months ago

  1. We should promote the event on Facebook, which is popular

I suggest promoting the event on social networking sites, especially Facebook, which is the most popular with people of all ages.

  1. I love my hometown which is peaceful.

I really love my hometown which is peaceful and stunning with a river and various kinds of flowers.

I really love my hometown where I was born and grew up with my beloved people.

  1. She is a beautiful girl who comes from England

She is a gorgeous girl who has long hair and black eyes.

  1. Halong Bay is a famous place which is beautiful

Halong Bay is a well-known travel destination which attracts millions of people to come here on holiday.

  1. I won the first prize, which surprised everyone
