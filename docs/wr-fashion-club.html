Writing – Fashion Club (đáp án mới)

FASHION CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a fashion club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What are you wearing today? – I am wearing a dress./ A T-shirt and jeans.

  2. How many people are there in your family? – 4 people./ Four people.

  3. What did you do yesterday? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  4. How do you go to school? – By car./ I walk to school.

  5. What's your hobby? – I like reading books./ My hobby is reading books.

Part 2: You are a new member of a fashion club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When was the last time you went shopping for clothes?

  • The last time I went shopping for clothes was last week. I bought a T-shirt and a pair of jeans from a shop. They were very beautiful.

  • Last week I bought a sweater and a jacket from a shop near my house. They cost 300 dollars, but I really liked them because they were fashionable and stylish.

Part 3: You are a member of a fashion club. You are talking to three other members of the club in the chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Some people like to buy clothes to try on themselves. Some prefer to buy online. How do you like it and why?

  • I prefer to buy online because it is convenient. I don't have to go to stores to buy clothes. I can stay at home and order, which can save me a lot of time.

  • I prefer buying clothes online for some reasons. First, it is more convenient as I don't have to go to stores, so I can save time. Second, I can buy things at low prices with discounts or vouchers.

B: Hi! Why did you decide to join the club?

  • I decided to join the club because I love fashion and I want to meet people who share my hobby. I think I can learn a lot of things from them.

  • I decided to take part in the club because I find a lot of interest in fashion and I would like to meet the club members who share my hobby.

C: Why do people like fashion?

  • People like fashion because it can make them look more beautiful and confident about themselves. Besides, it can show their personalities and creativity, and it also helps them express their own unique style.

  • People are interested in fashion for some reasons. The first reason is that fashion allows them to look better, so they feel more confident. Another reason is that it can show their creativity.

Part 4: You are a member of a fashion club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

The club is planning to hold meetings with famous designers, who will talk about their clothing designs and answer members' questions. We need your feedback on how to organise the event. Some people think it should be held online, while others want to organize a fashion-show-style event. Please state your opinion.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club is planning to hold meetings with famous designers, either online or as a fashion show. I think we should choose the online meetings because they're more convenient for everyone. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming event mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club is planning meetings with famous designers and needs feedback on whether to hold them online or as a fashion-show-style event. To be honest, I was very excited to hear that news.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I believe the meetings should be held online, since it is more convenient for designers who live in other countries and saves travel costs. Second, we should promote the event on Facebook, because it can reach more members and attract more interest.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
