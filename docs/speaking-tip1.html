How do you travel around?

  • I often travel by motorbike. It is fast and convenient. (miêu tả hành động)

  • I often travel by motorbike. It is fast and convenient. Sometimes I travel by bus. It is affordable. It is suitable for students and people with limited budgets. (nhân đôi thông tin)

  • I often travel by motorbike. It is fast and convenient. Sometimes I travel by bus. It is affordable. It is suitable for students and people with limited budgets. However, it is slow and not convenient (if we do not live near any bus stop). (đưa thêm thông đối ngược)

  • I often travel by motorbike. It is fast and convenient. Sometimes I travel by bus. It is affordable. It is suitable for students and people with limited budgets. However, it is slow and not convenient (if we do not live near any bus stop). I live near a bus stop and it is really convenient for me. It takes me only 5 minutes to walk there. (đưa thêm thông tin cá nhân)
