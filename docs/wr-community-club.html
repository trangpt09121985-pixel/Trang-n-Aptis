Writing – Community Club (đáp án mới)

COMMUNITY CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a Community club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Where do you live? – In Da Nang./ I live in Da Nang.

  2. What's your favourite kind of food? – Seafood./ I like seafood.

  3. What's your surname? – Nguyen./ My surname is Nguyen.

  4. What language do you speak? – Vietnamese./ I speak Vietnamese.

  5. What time do you often go to bed? – At 11 pm./ I go to bed at 11 pm.

Part 2: You are a new member of the Community club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Describe where you live.

  • I live in a small apartment in the city center, near a park. It is quiet and convenient, because there are many shops and a bus stop nearby.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: What do you usually do to help people around you?

  • I usually help my neighbors by carrying heavy things or looking after their children when they are busy. I also help elderly people cross the street or carry their bags sometimes.

B: I really like charity activities but don't know how, can you give me some advice?

  • I suggest starting with something simple, like donating clothes or books to a local charity. You could also join a volunteer group at a shelter, since it is an easy and meaningful way to begin.

C: Should teenagers volunteer during school holidays?

  • Yes, I think teenagers should volunteer during school holidays. It teaches them responsibility and empathy, and it also gives them valuable experience that can be useful for their studies and future careers.

Part 4: You are a member of a Community Club. You received this email from the club.

Dear Member,

The Community Club is running two community projects. The first is to provide local sports facilities for children and young people. The second is to build parks with large gardens for the elderly. Which project do you think the club should do and why?

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend, who is also a member of this club. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club is planning two community projects. I think we should choose the sports facilities project because it helps more young people. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the community club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the club is planning to choose between building sports facilities for young people or parks for the elderly. I was very interested to hear that news, because both projects sound very meaningful.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I think the club should choose the sports facilities project, because it can help children and young people improve their health, keep fit and prevent health problems. Second, the club could also call for support from the local government, since parks for the elderly could be built later with extra funding.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
