Informal, Formal letters, and 10 simple suggestions

  1. Informal letter

Hi Jane,

How’s it going? Did you hear anything about the upcoming plan? It’s amazing. I think we should have a talk show. What do you think? Let’s talk about it over coffee

Anyway, some coffee this weekend?

See you soon,

  1. Formal letter

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I have been an active member of the club. I am writing this email to share my ideas about the upcoming plan.

I have been informed that our club is going to encourage young people to cook at home. I was truly delighted to hear that because it was really useful for young people.

Being a member of the club, I would like to share some ideas. I think we can have a talk show and invite a famous chef to come and talk about the benefits of cooking at home. It is healthy and affordable. It helps them stay healthy and save money. In addition, it is a good idea to offer free cooking courses for them. It is practical and feasible to carry out.

Hopefully my ideas will be useful for our club.

Best regards,

  1. 10 simple suggestions

  • We should have a face-to-face or an online meeting for members to express their ideas directly. It is fast and easy to carry out

  • We should ask for help from an expert who has experience to deal with this. It is the best way

  • We should have a talk show and invite a famous author to come and talk about the benefits of reading books. It is practical and feasible to carry out

  • We should educate people about the importance of (art/environment..). It is the most effective in the long term

  • We should ask for help from the local government and organizations. It is fast and practical to carry out.

  • We should work with the local government and local schools to introduce about the …

  • We should promote the event on social networking sites such as Facebook because it can reach more people.

  • We should offer free tickets for local people. It is practical and easy to carry out.

  • We should not change anything because it can cause a lot of inconvenience for everybody and it may discourage members.

  • We should set clear rules about these behaviors. It is the most effective in the long term.
