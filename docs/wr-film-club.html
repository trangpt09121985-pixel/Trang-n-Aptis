Writing – Film Club (đáp án mới)

FILM CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a film club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Where are you from? – Vietnam./ I am from Vietnam.

  2. What do you like to do in the morning? – I like to read books./ Reading books.

  3. What is the weather like today? – It's sunny./ Sunny.

  4. When do you study? – In the evening./ I study in the evening.

  5. Do you like dancing? – Yes, I do./ Yes, I like dancing.

Part 2: You are a new member of a film club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When and where do you watch movies?

  • I watch movies in the living room with my family at the weekend or in the evening. It's fun and relaxing after a long, busy week.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Tell me the last time you watched a movie.

  • Yesterday I watched a movie with my friends at the cinema. It was a cartoon about a cat and a mouse. It was very funny, and I laughed from the beginning to the end.

B: When I watch films, I always fall asleep. What are useful ways to avoid falling asleep when watching movies?

  • To avoid falling asleep when watching movies, I think you should drink coffee or tea before the film starts. Also, you can eat some snacks or talk to other people to stay awake and focused.

C: I think films made in the past like black-and-white films are outdated and we shouldn't watch them. What do you think?

  • Your question is interesting, but I don't fully agree. I don't think we should avoid black-and-white films, because we can understand more about the technology, culture and life in the past through them.

Part 4: You are a member of the film club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

Our club is going to have a meeting for all members to attend. In this meeting, we will invite a famous film critic to present. Please give some suggestions about who we should invite and what topics we can talk about.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club is going to have a meeting and invite a famous film critic to speak. I think we should invite Mr. David and ask him to talk about "Movies Today". What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming meeting mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club is planning a meeting and will invite a famous film critic to give a talk. To be honest, I was so excited to hear that news, as I have been looking forward to an event like this for a long time.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I recommend inviting Mr. David, a well-known film critic in our city who has both experience and expertise. Second, I suggest the talk should focus on the topic "Movies Today", as it is interesting and can attract more members, both young and old.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
