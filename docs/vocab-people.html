Từ vựng liên quan đến người

  1. Personal information (name, age, from, family, job, hobby)

  • I’m going to talk about my best friend

  • Her name is Mary

  • She is 25 years old

  • She is from England

  • She is married

  • She has a son

  • She is a doctor

  • She works for a hospital in her hometown

  • She is a famous singer in Vietnam

  • She has strong voice

  • She is a famous film star in Vietnam

  • She has stared in many movies

  • In free time, she likes playing sports and reading books

  1. Appearance

  • She is beautiful with long hair and black eyes

  1. Personality

  • She is friendly and kind

  • She always helps me when I need

  1. Feeling

  • I really like her

Lượt 2: lặp lại lượt 1
