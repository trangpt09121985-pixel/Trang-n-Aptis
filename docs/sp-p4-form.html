NGỮ PHÁP: dùng thì quá khứ khi chia động từ

Verbs – Quá khứ đơn – có 3 loại động từ

  1. To be: was/ were

+ I/ He/ She/ It – was

VD: I was happy

VD: The class was dirty

+ They/You/We – were

VD: They were at home

  1. Normal Verbs: động từ thường

+ có quy tắc: cleaned, visited, cooked ..

VD: I visited my best friend last week

+ bất quy tắc: go – went, have – had, feel – felt, take – took, buy – bought, teach – taught, hear – heard, tell – told, say - said

VD: I felt excited when I heard the news

  1. Động từ khuyết thiếu: can – could, must – must, have to – had to, should – should, will – would

VD: I could not understand the lesson because it was difficult

  1. When was the last time you travelled by car? (lần gần đây nhất)

  • Thời gian: last week

VD: Last week I travelled by car

  • Làm gì: travelled by car

  • Đi đâu: went to HL bay

VD: I went to HL bay with my family

  • Đi với ai: with my family

  • Lúc di chuyển làm gì: During the trip, we talked and listened to music

  • Mât bao lâu: 2 hours

VD: It took me two hours from HN to HL bay

  • Cảm xúc: happy (relax, reduce stress, share happy moments with my family, forget all the troubles)

VD: I felt really excited because I could share happy moments with my family

Last week I travelled by car. I went to HL bay with my family. During the trip, we talked and listened to music. It took me two hours to travel from HN to HL bay. I felt very happy because I could share happy moments with my family

  1. When was the last time you ate out with your friends?

  • Khi nào: las week

  • Làm gì: ate out with my friends

  • Với ai

  • ở đâu: in a restaurant near my house

  • chỗ ăn ntn: big and beautiful

  • đồ ăn ntn: delicious, healthy, cheap/expensive/affordable

  • ăn gì: seafood, salad, soup

  • thích món gì: soup – tasty

  • cảm xúc: happy – relax, share happy moments with my friends

Last week I ate out with my friends in a restaurant near my house. It was big and beautiful. The food was delicious, healthy and cheap. We ate seafood, salad and soup. I really liked the soup because it was tasty. I felt very happy because I could relax and share happy moments with my friends.
