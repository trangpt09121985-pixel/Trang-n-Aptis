VOCAB FOR WRITING

(interest, value - sự quan tâm, thích thú, và giá trị mà clb đem lại cho cá nhân, cộng đồng)

  • Like, love, enjoy + V-ing

VD: I love reading books

  • Be interested in: yêu thích, quan tâm

VD: I am interested in reading books

  • Be into : yêu thích

VD: I am into reading books

  • Find a lot of interest in: tìm thấy nhiều niềm thích thú trong..

VD: I find a lot of interest in reading books

  • Find (reading books) enjoyable and useful: thấy đọc sách thú vụ và hữu ích

VD: I find reading books/it enjoyable and useful for children

  • …is my cup of tea: …là niềm yêu thích của tôi

VD: Reading books is my cup of tea

  • Be passionate about ….: đam mê về

VD: I am passionate about sports

  • (playing sports) is one of the best ways to improve both my mental and physical health

  • Improve my mental health: tăng cường sk tinh thần

(= Enhance my mental well-being)

VD: Reading books helps me improve my mental health and broaden my mind

  • Improve my physical health: cải thiện sk thể chất

(= Enhance my physical well-being)

VD: It helps me improve my physical health, keep fit and prevent health problems

  • Broaden my mind: mở mang kiến thức

  • Update information: cập nhật thông tin

  • Stay updated: cập nhật thông tin

VD: Watching TV helps me stay updated

  • …affordable …: không đắt

  • Save money for other important things

  • ….suitable for people with low-income and limited budgets

VD: It is affordable for people with low-income and limited budgets. It helps people save money for other important things

  • Improve many skills such as communication and language skills

VD: Community activities help children improve many skills such as communication and language skills

  • Inspire/motivate (young people) to cook at home: thôi thúc ai làm gì

VD: The talk show can inspire/motivate young people to read books

  • Be beneficial for ….: có lợi cho ..

VD: I believe that this plan/activity is beneficial for people who are interested in music

VD: Do you like reading books?

  • Yes, I do. I really love reading books because it is enjoyable and useful. It helps me relax after work and improve my knowledge.

  • Yes, I do. I am really interested in reading books, especially science books. It is one of the best way to enhance my mental well-being and broaden my mind. It is perfect for people who would like to improve their vocabulary and learn new things.

  • Find a lot of interest in

  • Find ……+ adj (interesting and useful)

  • be one of the best ways to ….

  • be beneficial for (tương đối có lợi cho) …..
