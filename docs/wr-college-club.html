Writing – College Club (đáp án mới)

COLLEGE CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a college. You have 5 messages from a student at the college. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your surname? – My surname is Nguyen./ Nguyen.

  2. How did you come here? – By bus./ I came here by bus.

  3. What language do you speak? – I speak Vietnamese./ Vietnamese.

  4. What is your favourite subject? – English./ I like English.

  5. Who is your best friend? – Lan./ My best friend is Lan.

Part 2: You are a new student at the college. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Please tell us about the days and times you can attend courses and what you would like to study.

  • I am free in the afternoons, and I can attend courses on Monday and Tuesday afternoon. I would like to study English, and I hope this course can help me.

Part 3: You are talking to other students at the college. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Hi! Welcome to college. What course are you doing and why did you choose it?

  • I am studying English. I chose this course because it is very important for my future job, and if I can speak English well, I can communicate with people all over the world.

B: Welcome! How do you feel about the college so far?

  • I think the college is great. I am learning numerous useful things from well-qualified teachers and making friends with smart and studious students. I really like the environment here.

C: Did you hear that the college sports club has closed? What do you think about this?

  • I know that the college sports club has closed, and this is bad news for me and many others, because that is where we could play sports and share happy moments together.

Part 4: You are a member of a college. You received this email from the college administrator.

Dear student,

We are writing to inform you that the end-of-course exams will now take place two weeks earlier than originally planned. Your exam, which was scheduled for the 21st of next month, will now take place on the 7th of next month. We apologize for any difficulty this may cause.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your feelings and what you plan to do. Write about 50 words.

Hi Dan,

I just heard that our exams have been moved two weeks earlier, from the 21st to the 7th of next month. I think we should start revising together right away. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you soon
  1. Write an email to the college administrator. Tell them your opinion and suggestions. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Administrator,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming exam schedule mentioned in the recent email from the college.

I have been informed that the end-of-course exams will now take place two weeks earlier, moving from the 21st to the 7th of next month. To be honest, I was quite worried to hear that news, as students now have less time to prepare.

Being a student of the college, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should have an online meeting for students to express their concerns directly to the administration, as it is fast and easy to carry out. Second, we should ask lecturers to provide extra revision sessions before the new exam date, as this would help students prepare more effectively.

I hope my ideas will be useful to the college.

Best regards
