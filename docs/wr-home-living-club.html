Writing – Home living Club (đáp án mới)

HOME LIVING CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a Home Living club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Where do you live? – In Hanoi./ I live in Hanoi.

  2. What is your favourite room in your house? – The living room./ I like the living room.

  3. How many people live with you? – 4 people./ There are 4 people.

  4. What is your favourite colour for a room? – Blue./ I like blue.

  5. What time do you usually get home? – At 6 pm./ I get home at 6 pm.

Part 2: You are a new member of the Home Living club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Describe where you live.

  • I live in a house in Hanoi. There are 5 rooms: a living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom. It is small but beautiful, and I really like it.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: You have to stay in the bedroom but have to share it with your brother. How do you feel?

  • I feel happy because it is fun and useful. We can talk and share everything in life. Also, it is economical, because we can save both money and space.

B: It is better to live in the city than in the countryside. Do you agree? Why?

  • I don't agree with this idea. In the countryside, the air is fresh and good for our health. The food is delicious and cheap, and the people are friendly and kind.

C: Nowadays, old buildings are demolished and replaced with modern buildings. I feel we should protect historic buildings. What do you think?

  • I think some old buildings are not safe for the people living there, so they should be demolished and replaced with modern ones. New buildings are usually more beautiful and comfortable.

Part 4: You are a member of the Home Living club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

Currently, there are many young families who want to buy a house or an apartment. The club wants members to make suggestions to help them find homes.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club wants to help young families find houses or apartments to buy. I think we should connect them with local real estate agents. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to share my opinions about the upcoming plan mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that many young families are looking for a house to buy, and our club wants members to help them. To be honest, I was very happy to hear that news, because it is a meaningful project.

I would like to offer two ideas. First, we should hold an online meeting for members to share useful information, as it is fast and easy to carry out. Second, we should ask an expert in real estate for help, since this is the best way to give reliable advice.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
