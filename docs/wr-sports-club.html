Writing – Sports Club (kèm gợi ý đáp án)

SPORTS CLUB

(kèm gợi ý đáp án)

Part 1: You want to join a Sports Club. Fill in the form. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each question. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

1. What's the weather like today? – Sunny and warm / It's raining

2. How are you? – I'm fine, thanks / Pretty good

3. What's your favorite hobby? – Playing football / Reading books

4. How do you get to work? – By bus / By bike

5. What do you like to do in the evening? – Watching TV / Going for a walk

Part 2: You are a new member of the Sports Club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Why did you decide to join the club?

- I decided to join the sports club because I want to improve my health and make new friends. I also enjoy playing sports in my free time.

Part 3: You are a member of a Sports Club. You are talking to three other members in the sports club chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words.

A: When was the last time you played sports?

- I played football with my friends last weekend. We played for about two hours at the park near my house. It was great fun and good exercise for all of us.

B: I usually play sports with my friends and watch sports programs. What about you?

- I usually play sports alone because it helps me relax and focus. However, I also enjoy watching sports programs on TV, especially football matches, with my family at the weekend.

C: I like playing sports with my friend but my brother likes playing sports alone. What do you think about this issue?

- I think both are ok, depending on people’s interests. Playing with friends is more fun and social, while playing alone helps you focus better. Both ways are good for your health.

Part 4: You are a member of the Sports Club. You received this email from the club.

Dear members,

The club needs suggestions about the organization of a sports competition for young members.

1. Write an email to your friend. (about 50 words)

Hi Tom,

How's it going? I just heard that our club is going to organize a sports competition for young members. I think we should have a talk show and invite a well-known athlete to talk about the benefits of playing sports. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later.

Lan

2. Write an email to the manager of the club. (120-150 words)

Dear Manager,

My name is Lan. I have been an active member of the club for the last six months. I am writing this email to express my ideas about the upcoming sports competition for young members mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that the club is going to organize a sports competition for young members. To be honest, I was truly delighted to hear that news.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should have a talk show and invite a well-known athlete to come and talk about the benefits of playing sports. It is practical and feasible to carry out. Second, we should promote the event on social networking sites such as Facebook because it can reach more people.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards,

Lan
