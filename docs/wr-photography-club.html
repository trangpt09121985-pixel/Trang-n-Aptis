Writing – Photography Club (đáp án mới)

PHOTOGRAPHY CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a photography club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Do you like shopping? – Yes, I do./ Yes, I like shopping.

  2. What do you usually do in the morning? – Check my emails./ I usually check my emails.

  3. What is your favorite animal? – Dogs./ I like dogs.

  4. Where did you go last night? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  5. What is your favorite time of year? – Summer./ I like summer.

Part 2: You are a new member of the photography club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When and where do you like taking photographs?

  • I like taking photographs early in the morning at the beach, when the light is soft and beautiful. I also enjoy taking photos during family trips.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Sajed: My favorite family photograph is a picture of me when I was a child. Can you tell me about one of your family photos?

  • My favorite family photo is a picture of my grandparents' wedding day. It is very old, but I love it because it shows how happy and young they looked at that time.

Jo: I enjoy taking photos of people, especially if they are doing something active. What kind of photographs do you like taking and why?

  • I enjoy taking photos of nature, especially mountains and rivers during my trips. I like these photos because they remind me of the peaceful and beautiful places I have visited.

Chris: People are always taking photos of themselves with their phones these days. Do you think this is a good thing? Why or why not?

  • I think it depends on the situation. Taking photos of yourself can be a nice way to remember special moments, but doing it too often can also be a little bit annoying.

Part 4: You are a member of the photography club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear Member,

As you know, every year we hold a competition for the best photograph from our members. In the past we have had just one single category. This year we are thinking about having separate categories for men and women and another category for people under 16. Before we make any changes, we would like to hear from you. What do you think of the proposed change? Please do not forget to tell us the reason for your opinion.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write about 50 words. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club may add separate photo categories for men, women and people under 16. I think it's a great idea. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the president of the club. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write 120-150 words. Recommended time: 20 minutes.

Dear President,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the photography club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the proposed change mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the club is thinking about having separate competition categories instead of just one. I was very pleased to hear that news, because I think it is a fair and positive change.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I support the new categories, because it gives everyone, especially younger photographers, a fairer chance to win. Second, this change could also encourage more women and young people to join the competition.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
