Cách miêu tả tranh P2

(By Ms Mai Hien)

Family, park, play football, casual, happy, backg, weather, feeling

  • người: nhìn thấy ai, đoán mối quan hệ

Vd: In the picture, I can see some people. I think they are a family

VD: In the picture, I can see two people a man and a child. The man is about 40 and the child is about 10. I guess/ It seems that they are father and son

  • họ đang ở đâu: at home, in a restaurant, in a forest, in the park, in the field

VD: They are at home

  • họ đang làm gì (hoạt động, trang phục) – be + V-ing

VD: They are playing football

VD: The woman is reading a newspaper. The man is working on a laptop

VD: The woman is going to kick the ball. The man is going to catch the ball

VD: It seems that/ Maybe they have just left a shopping center

VD: They are wearing casual clothes/ formal clothes/ uniform

  • Trông họ ntn

VD: They look happy/ excited/focused/ serious.

VD: He looks excited and I guess the game is interesting

VD: They are laughing so I think the movie is funny

VD: They look happy so I think they are enjoying their time

  • Đồ vật xung quanh

VD: On the table, I can see some food and drink

VD: On the wall, I can see some pictures

VD: Behind them, I can see some plants

VD: Around them, I can see many trees

VD: Next to them, I can see a cat and a dog

VD: On the floor, I can see a hat

VD: On the ground, I can see a lot of snow.

  • Background

VD: In the background of the picture, I can see a mountain and many trees

  • Thời tiết

+ The weather in the picture is very nice. It is sunny and perfect for outdoor activities.

+ The weather in this picture is very cold because I can see a lot of snow on the ground and the people are wearing warm clothes.

+ The weather is this picture is very hot. The sun is shining brightly

  • Cảm xúc của người miêu tả: I really like this picture

+ Because it shows happiness of a family

+ Because it shows love between father and son/people and animals

+ because it reminds me of my father/childhood. When I was small, I usually played football with my father

+ because it reminds me of my friends. In free time, we often go out together.

  • Nhìn thấy ai? (bao nhiêu người, tuổi, mối quan hệ)

VD: In the first picture, I can see two people, a man and a woman. They are about 40. I think they are a couple

  • Họ đang ở đâu

VD: They are eating in a restaurant

  • Họ đang làm gì?

VD: They are sitting around the table and talking

  • Trông họ như thế nào?

VD: They look happy/focused/serious
