Speaking P4 – dịch đề (32 bộ câu hỏi)

Speaking P4

STT
1

  1. Tell me about a time when you used technology to solve a problem.

  2. What are the advantages of using technology in daily life?

  3. Do you think people rely too much on technology nowadays? Why or why not?
2

  1. Tell me about a time when you learned a new skill.

  2. How did you feel when you successfully mastered that skill?

  3. What is the best way for people to learn and improve new skills in their daily life?
3

  1. Tell me about a time when you attended a memorable sports event.

  2. Why do you think some sports events attract millions of viewers worldwide?

  3. Do you agree that hosting international sports events is beneficial for a country?
4

  1. Tell me about a time when you visited a historical building.

  2. How did you feel when you were there?

  3. Why do you think it is important to preserve historical sites for future generations?
5

  1. Tell me about a time when you successfully completed a task.

  2. How did you feel after completing that task?

  3. Why is it important to complete tasks successfully?
6

1. Tell me a time when you had an interesting conversation with someone.

2. How did you feel about it?

3. What should we do to have a good conversation?
7

1. Tell me about a memorable experience you had related to an important cultural event.

2. What do people usually do during important cultural events in your country?

3. Do you think cultural events are important for people? Why or why not?
8

  1. Tell me about a time when you had to face a difficult challenge in your life.

  2. How did your feelings change before and after you overcame that challenge?

  3. What are the benefits for people when they face and conquer challenges?
9

  1. Tell me about a time when someone helped you.

  2. How did you feel at that time?

  3. Why is it important to help others?
10

  1. Tell me about a time when you met or talked to a foreigner.

  2. Why do some people feel shy or nervous when communicating with strangers from other countries?

  3. Do you think interacting with people from different cultural backgrounds is important?
11

  1. Tell me about a time you learned something new.

  2. What do you think about the belief that older people don't learn anything new? Is it correct?

  3. What are your thoughts on the idea that it's never too late, whether you're young or old, to start learning a new skill?
12

  1. Tell me about a time when you were in a hurry.

  2. How did you feel?

  3. If you're running late for something, how do you typically feel?
13

  1. Tell me about a time when you wanted to change yourself.

  2. Why did you want to make that change?

  3. Do you think changing yourself is important for personal growth?
14

  1. Tell me about a time when you read an interesting book.

  2. Why do some people prefer reading books to watching movies?

  3. Do you think e-books will completely replace printed books in the future? Why or why not?
15

  1. Tell me about a time when you had possession of something valuable?

  2. Why was this possession important to you and how did you feel when you had it?

  3. Why do you think people should share their possessions with others?
16

  1. Tell me about a time you asked a question.

  2. Why do some people feel nervous to ask questions in public?

  3. How does asking questions help students learn better at school?
17

  1. Tell me about a time when you worked in a team.

  2. How do you feel when you work in a team?

  3. What are the advantages of teamwork?
18

  1. Tell me about a time when you wore an important outfit for a special occasion.

  2. Why do people like to dress up or wear formal clothes on special events?

  3. Do you think school uniforms or workplace dress codes are necessary?
19

1. Tell me about a time when you visited a historical building. (toà nhà lịch sử, cổ)

2. How did you feel when you were there?

3. Why do you think it is important to preserve historical sites for future generations?
20

1. Tell me about a time when you had to plan for something important.

2. How did you feel during the planning process? (trong suốt quá trình lên kế hoạch)

3. Why do you think planning is important in our lives?
21

1. Tell me about a time when you received negative feedback.

2. How did you feel about that feedback?

3. Do you think negative feedback is helpful for people?
23

1. Tell me about a time when you were responsible for something important.

2. How did you feel during that experience?

3. Why is being responsible important in life?
24

  1. Tell me about a time you received a gift from someone.

  2. How do you feel when you receive a gift?

  3. A handmade present is better than a present bought from a shop. What do you think?
25

  1. Tell me about a time when you couldn’t buy the thing that you want?

  2. How did you feel that time?

  3. Material possession alone cannot make people happy. Do you agree/ disagree?
26

  1. Describe a time you visited someone?

  2. What do you think about having unexpected guests?

  3. Some people prefer living in small communities while others prefer living in big cities. What are your thoughts?
27

1. Tell me about the last time you and your friends laughed together.

2. How did you feel at that moment?

3. People laugh at different things. What do you think about that?
28

  1. Tell me a time when you received good news

  2. How did you feel?

  3. Why do people like to celebrate good news?
29

  1. Tell me about a time you changed your school or your job.

  2. How did you feel at that time?

  3. Do you think that change is good for society?
30

1. Tell me about a time you were with someone older or younger than you.

2. How did you feel in this situation?

3. How do you think different generations can learn from each other?
31

  1. Tell me about a time when you had to make a choice.

  2. How do you feel when you have to make a difficult decision?

  3. Can you give some general advice to people who have to make a choice?
32

1. Tell me about a time when you tried hard to achieve your goal?

2. How do you feel about it?

3. What do people need to do to achieve their goals
