Writing – Music Club 2 (đáp án mới)

MUSIC CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a music club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. Where were you born? – I was born in Hanoi./ In Hanoi.

  2. What is your hobby? – I love reading books./ My hobby is playing sports.

  3. When do you usually have breakfast? – At 8 am./ I have breakfast at 8 am.

  4. How old are you? – I'm 40 years old./ 40 years old.

  5. How often do you go shopping? – Twice a week./ I go shopping twice a week.

Part 2: You are a new member of a music club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When and where do you usually listen to music?

  • I usually listen to music at home in the evening. Sometimes I listen to music in a coffee shop at the weekend.

  • I often enjoy music with some close friends in a nice café in the city at the weekend. It's enjoyable, and it helps me relax after a busy week.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the music club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: My grandmother often sang to me when I was little. She had a beautiful voice. Tell me about a good memory of yours.

  • When I was small, I lived with my grandmother. She often read stories to me before I went to bed. I really loved the stories she read. They were very interesting and useful.

  • When I was small, I lived with my aunt, and she took care of me. She often read stories to me before bedtime. I really loved the fairy stories she read. They were very interesting and meaningful.

B: Playing loud music annoys people around you. What is your opinion?

  • I agree with this idea, because it can badly affect many people. Some people cannot bear loud music, and some are trying to focus on their work. Others may want to sleep.

  • I totally agree with this idea, because it can seriously affect people around us, especially elderly people and children. Some cannot bear loud music, and others are trying to concentrate on their work.

C: Music taste differs with each generation. What is your opinion?

  • I strongly agree with this idea. In my country, older people often listen to classical music or bolero songs, while young people love rock, pop or remix music.

  • I strongly agree with this idea, because people of different ages love different kinds of music. Older people often listen to classical or country music, while young people are more interested in pop or rock music.

Part 4: You are a member of the music club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

Our club is going to organise a music concert, and we want to attract both the young and the old. Please give your opinions.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club is going to organise a music concert for both young and old members. I think we should offer free tickets to attract more people. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming music concert mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that our club is going to organise a music concert to attract both young and old members. To be honest, I was very excited to hear that news, as this will be our first big event.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should offer free tickets or a big discount for families, including grandparents, parents and children, as this is practical and easy to do. Second, it is advisable for us to vary the music played, mixing classical and modern songs, so that everyone can enjoy the concert.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
