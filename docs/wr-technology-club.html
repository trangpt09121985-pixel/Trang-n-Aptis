Writing – Technology Club (đáp án mới)

TECHNOLOGY CLUB

Question 1: You want to join a Technology club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes. Example: How are you? I'm fine, thanks.

  1. What is your job? – I'm a student./ A student.

  2. What is your favorite color? – Green./ It's green.

  3. What is your favorite food? – Pizza./ I like pizza.

  4. What do you usually do in the morning? – Check my emails./ I usually check my emails.

  5. Where did you go last night? – I stayed at home./ Home.

Question 2: You are a new member of the Technology club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

What do you usually use a computer for?

  • I usually use a computer for studying and doing research for school. I also use it to watch videos, chat with friends and sometimes play online games.

Question 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Have you ever had a bad experience with technology?

  • Yes, once my laptop suddenly stopped working right before an important deadline. I was very stressed, but luckily I had saved my work online, so I did not lose anything important.

B: Which do you prefer, watching films online or going to the cinema?

  • I prefer watching films online because it is more convenient and cheaper. I can watch whenever I want, and I don't have to travel to the cinema or wait in a queue.

C: Improvement in technology has made life become easier. What do you think?

  • I agree that technology has made life easier in many ways, such as communication and work. However, I think it can also make people less active and more dependent on machines.

Question 4: You are a member of the Technology Club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear member,

Members are active online by posting and commenting on posts. However, some members are commenting negative (tiêu cực) things about others. The postings on our system are anonymous (hệ thống ẩn danh) so we are thinking about changing the system to identify (định danh) who comments. What do you think? Do you have any alternative method? Provide your suggestions and reasons.

From the manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend who is also a member to talk about your suggestions.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club is thinking about changing the online system so that comments are no longer anonymous. I think it is a good idea. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to your manager about your suggestions and reasons. (120-150 words)

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the technology club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, some members are posting negative comments anonymously on our system. I was quite disappointed to hear that news, because it is not a respectful way to communicate.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I think we should change the system so that everyone's name is shown, because people will think twice before posting something negative. Second, we should also set clear rules about acceptable online behavior, since this will be the most effective solution in the long term.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
