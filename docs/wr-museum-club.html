Writing – Museum Club (đáp án mới)

MUSEUM CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a museum club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is the weather like today? – Sunny and warm./ It's sunny and warm.

  2. What are you wearing today? – A blue shirt./ I'm wearing a blue shirt.

  3. What is your favorite kind of music? – Pop music./ I like pop music.

  4. Which sport is the most popular in your country? – Football./ I like football.

  5. What do you like doing with your friend? – Watching movies./ I like watching movies.

Part 2: You are a new member of the museum club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Tell us about the last time you went to a museum.

  • Last month, I went to the History Museum with my class. We saw many interesting old objects, and our teacher explained the history behind each one.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: I went to the museum at school. How was the time when you visited the museum when you were a child?

  • When I was a child, I visited the museum with my school every year. I remember feeling amazed by the huge dinosaur bones, and I always looked forward to those school trips.

B: I want to take my children to a museum but I don't know which, the History Museum or Sports Museum. Can you give me advice?

  • I think the History Museum would be a better choice for your children, because it has more interactive activities and colorful displays that are usually more interesting for young children.

C: Some museums have added technologies like computer screens in their museum and this attracts more visitors. Why?

  • I think technology attracts more visitors because it makes learning more interesting and fun. For example, computer screens can show videos and games that explain the history behind each object.

Part 4: You are a member of the museum club. You have received this email from the club.

Dear members,

The museums are free of charge but next year museums will require tickets and this might decrease visitors. What do you think? Make suggestions and explain.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write about 50 words. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our museum may start charging an entrance fee next year. I think we should keep it free to avoid losing visitors. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the president of the club. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write 120-150 words. Recommended time: 20 minutes.

Dear President,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the museum club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the museum may start charging tickets next year, which could decrease the number of visitors. I was quite worried to hear that news, since the museum is currently very popular because it is free.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some suggestions. First, I think we should not charge a fee, because it may cause inconvenience and discourage regular visitors. Second, if money is really needed, we could simply ask for optional donations instead, since this is a fair and practical solution.

Hopefully, my suggestions will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
