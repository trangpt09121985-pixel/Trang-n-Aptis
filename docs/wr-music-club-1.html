Writing – Music Club 1 (đáp án mới)

MUSIC CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a music club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What's your favourite sport? – I love tennis./ Tennis.

  2. How many people are there in your family? – There are 4 people./ 4 people.

  3. What did you do yesterday? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  4. How do you go to school? – By bus./ I go to school by bus.

  5. What's your hobby? – It is reading books./ Reading books.

Part 2: You are a new member of a music club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Please tell me the last time you listened to music.

  • The last time I listened to music was last night when I came home from work. I listened to a song which is very popular right now.

Part 3: You are a member of the music club. You are talking to three other members in the music club chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Hi! How often do you listen to music?

  • I listen to music every day, as it is really relaxing and enjoyable. I often listen to music with my friends in a café near my house. They often play my favourite songs.

B: I plan to give my son music lessons, but he doesn't like it. What should I do?

  • You could talk to your son about the benefits of learning music, or take him to a café or theatre where they play music. I think he will like it once he tries.

C: Do you agree that music reflects cultural differences?

  • I agree that music reflects cultural differences. It is true that when we hear a piece of music, we can often guess its country of origin, because it can serve as a symbol of a culture.

Part 4: You are a member of the music club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

Recently, our club has held some concerts and played music at night. However, residents living near our club have complained about the noise from our concerts. Please give some suggestions to solve this problem.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Tom,

I just heard that residents living near our club have been complaining about the noise from our concerts. I think we should hold the concerts earlier in the evening instead. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the noise problem mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that residents living near our club have complained about the noise from our concerts. To be honest, I was quite worried to hear that news, as it could damage our club's reputation.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should have an online meeting so members can discuss possible solutions directly, which is fast and easy to carry out. Second, it is advisable for us to install soundproofing devices or hold concerts in a venue further from residential areas, so as not to disturb the neighbourhood.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
