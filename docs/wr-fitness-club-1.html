Writing – Fitness Club 1 (đáp án mới)

FITNESS CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a fitness club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. How are you? – I'm fine, thanks./ Fine, thanks.

  2. Which is your favorite season? – Summer./ I like summer.

  3. What is the weather like today? – Sunny and hot./ It's sunny and hot.

  4. What is your favorite sport? – Swimming./ I like swimming.

  5. What do you do on the weekend? – Playing football./ I like playing football.

Part 2: You are a new member of the fitness club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Why did you decide to join the club?

  • I decided to join the club because I want to keep fit and lose some weight. I also want to make new friends who enjoy exercising too.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Person A: Tell me something you do to keep fit.

  • To keep fit, I usually go jogging every morning before work. I also try to eat healthy food and drink a lot of water every day. Sometimes I play badminton with my friends at the weekend too.

Person B: Do you prefer getting up in the morning to go for a run?

  • Yes, I prefer getting up early to go for a run. The air is fresh in the morning, and it also helps me feel more energetic for the rest of the day.

Person C: Technology makes our lives easier, therefore we are too lazy to do physical exercises. What is your opinion?

  • I agree that technology makes life easier, but I don't think we are too lazy because of it. Many people still make time to exercise, even with a busy schedule.

Part 4: You are a member of the Fitness club. You have received this email from the club:

Dear members,

The club equipment is too old and needs to be replaced. The club plans to organize a sponsor event to raise money or increase the membership fee by 50%. What are your opinions and suggestions?

Bests, Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write about 50 words. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that the club equipment is too old and the club may increase the membership fee by 50%. I think we should organize a sponsor event instead. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the president of the club. Write about your feelings and what you think the club should do about the situation. Write 120-150 words. Recommended time: 20 minutes.

Dear President,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the fitness club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, our club equipment is too old and needs to be replaced. I was quite worried to hear that news, especially about the plan to increase the membership fee by 50%.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I think we should organize a sponsor event instead of raising the fee, because a higher fee may discourage many members from staying in the club. Second, we could ask local businesses for help, since it is a fast and practical way to raise money for new equipment.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
