Writing – Food Club (đáp án mới)

FOOD CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a Food club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your favorite animal? – Dogs./ I like dogs.

  2. Where did you go last night? – I stayed at home./ Home.

  3. What is your favorite time of year? – Summer./ I like summer.

  4. What is your job? – I'm a student./ A student.

  5. What is your favorite subject in high school? – English./ I like English.

Part 2: You are a new member of the Food club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When and where do you usually eat meals?

  • I usually eat my meals at home with my family in the evening. Sometimes on weekends, I go out to eat at a restaurant with my friends instead.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: Tell me about a time you ate a nice meal?

  • Last month I had a delicious seafood dinner at a small restaurant near the beach with my family. The food was fresh and tasty, and we all really enjoyed the meal together.

B: Tell me about a place to eat local food (đồ ăn địa phương)? What food?

  • I recommend a small street food stall near my house that sells banh mi. The bread is crispy, and the filling is always fresh, so it is very popular with local people.

C: People are likely to eat more and more fast food. It is unhealthy and they should eat less. What do you think?

  • I agree that fast food is not very healthy, but many people still eat it because it is quick and convenient. I think people should try to cook more meals at home.

Part 4: You are a member of the Food Club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

We are planning a series of meetings on Wednesday, we need volunteers to have a short talk about food. What will you talk about? Why do you think it is interesting?

  1. Write an email to your friend, who is also a member of this club. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club needs volunteers to give a short talk about food. I think I will talk about street food in Vietnam because it's interesting. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the food club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming talks mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the club needs volunteers to give a short talk about food. I was very excited to hear that news, because I love sharing my ideas about food with other members.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I would like to talk about street food in Vietnam, because it is cheap, tasty and part of our culture. Second, I could also talk about healthy eating habits, since it is a useful topic for everyone in the club.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
