Writing – Reading Club (đáp án mới)

READING CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a Reading club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. How is the weather today? – Sunny and warm./ It's sunny and warm.

  2. What is your favorite season of the year? – Autumn./ I like autumn.

  3. What do you like to do every morning? – Reading the news./ I like reading the news.

  4. What do you like to do in your free time? – Reading books./ I like reading books.

  5. What is your job? – I'm a teacher./ A teacher.

Part 2: You are a new member of the Reading club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

When was the last time you read a book and how was the book?

  • I read a mystery novel last weekend. It was really exciting, and I finished it in just two days because I could not stop thinking about the ending.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

Person A: I like reading sports magazines. What genre of books do you like to read? Why?

  • I enjoy reading mystery and adventure novels because they keep me curious about what will happen next. I also like biographies, since they teach me interesting lessons from other people's real lives.

Person B: I have a lot of books for my studies, but I can't remember everything I read. Do you have any suggestions for me?

  • I suggest taking short notes or highlighting important points while you read. Reviewing your notes once a week can also help you remember the information for a much longer time.

Person C: Children nowadays prefer playing games to reading books. Do you think this is worrying, why?

  • Yes, I think it is a little worrying, because reading helps develop imagination and knowledge. However, parents can encourage children by choosing fun, colorful books that are easy to enjoy.

Part 4: You are a member of the Reading club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

The club was invited to contribute to the design of the national library. Most people say that the library is a boring place and don't use it often. We need to make the library a modern and exciting place that attracts both young and old. Please contribute your suggestions.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend, who is also a member of this club. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club wants to help redesign the national library. I think we should suggest adding a cozy reading corner with comfortable chairs. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the reading club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my ideas about the situation mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, the library needs to become more modern and exciting to attract both young and old visitors. I was very excited to hear that news, because I think this is a great opportunity to change how people see libraries.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, the library could add a cozy reading corner with comfortable chairs and soft lighting, to make it more relaxing. Second, it could also organize free workshops and book clubs for different age groups, since this would attract more visitors regularly.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
