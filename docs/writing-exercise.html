Writing – Exercise Club

Exercise Club

Part 4.

The building where the club holds weekly meetings is old and in need of

repair. There are some suggestions as

follows:

- Organize a running competition to raise funds for the club.

- Increase membership fees.

1. Write a letter to a friend (also a member of the club), giving your thoughts

on this issue.

2. Write a letter to the club manager, giving your opinion on the above

suggestions and providing additional solutions for the club.
