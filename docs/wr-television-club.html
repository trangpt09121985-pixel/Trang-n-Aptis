Writing – Television Club (đáp án mới)

TELEVISION CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a Television club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message.

  1. What are you wearing today? – A blue shirt./ I'm wearing a blue shirt.

  2. What is your favorite kind of music? – Pop music./ I like pop music.

  3. Which sport is the most popular in your country? – Football./ I like football.

  4. What is your favorite animal? – Dogs./ I like dogs.

  5. What is the weather like today? – Sunny and warm./ It's sunny and warm.

Part 2: You are a new member of a Television club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words.

When do you watch TV? What do you usually watch?

  • I usually watch TV in the evening after dinner. I mostly watch the news and some comedy shows, because they help me relax after a long day.

Part 3: You are talking to other members of the club in the chat room. Talk to them using sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer.

A: I usually sit on a comfortable chair and eat something while watching TV. How do you relax when watching TV?

  • I usually relax while watching TV by lying on my bed with some snacks. It helps me forget about work and enjoy some free time before going to sleep.

B: Which do you prefer: watch alone or with others?

  • I prefer watching TV with my family. It is more fun to share opinions about the show together, and we can also spend some quality time as a family.

C: Children shouldn't watch too much TV. Do you think so?

  • I agree that children shouldn't watch too much TV. It is better for them to spend more time reading, playing outside and studying instead of sitting in front of a screen.

Part 4: You are a member of the Television club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear member,

The Television Club is going to hold an annual meeting. Recommend when and where to hold.

The manager,

  1. Write an email to your friend, who is also a member of the group. Tell your friend about how you feel about the situation and what should be done. 50 words.

Hi Jane,

It's me, Linda. How's it going? I just heard that our club is going to hold an annual meeting. I think we should hold it on a Saturday evening at the community hall. What do you think? Let's talk about it later.

See you soon.
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion. 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Linda. I have been an active member of the television club for 6 months. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the upcoming annual meeting mentioned in the recent email from our club.

According to the email, our club is planning to hold an annual meeting soon. I was very happy to hear that news, because it is a great chance for members to meet each other.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, I think we should hold the meeting on a Saturday evening, because most members are free to attend at that time. Second, it is better to hold it at the community hall near the club, since it is convenient and easy for everyone to find.

Hopefully, my ideas will be useful for our club. I am looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards
