Buổi 6 – 5 mẹo phát triển ý

Buổi 6: 5 Mẹo phát triển ý

  1. Đưa thêm thông tin: làm gì, với ai, vào thời gian nào, tại sao làm việc đó, làm ở đâu, cảm xúc

  • Tại sao làm việc gì đó?

+ It is + adj (fun, enjoyable, interesting, convenient, popular, fast, fresh, friendly, kind, delicious, healthy, useful)

+ It helps me relax after work and broaden my mind

  • Feeling: I really like it. It’s important to me

Eg1: What do you often do after dinner?

I often watch TV with my family. It is fun and useful. It helps me relax after a long day at work and reduce stress. Also, it allows me to share happy moments with my family and learn a lot of things. I really like it. Sometimes I go to a café with my friends. Here we can drink coffee, listen to music and talk about everything. It’s relaxing

Eg2: How often do you go shopping?

  • I go shopping once a week. I often go shopping with my friends at the weekend. It’s fun and useful. It helps reduce stress and share happy moments with my friends. When I am busy, I go shopping once a month. Sometimes I shop online. It is convenient. It helps me save time.

  1. Nhân đôi thông tin (Vd: hỏi lúc rảnh bạn làm gì? – tôi chơi thể thao, đi cà phê)

Play spo, - frie- we – fun, good – rel, imp – caf -

Egg: What do you often do in your free time?

In my free time, I play sports. I often play tennis with my wife at the weekend. It’s fun and useful. It helps me relax after work and improve my health. Sometimes I go to a café with my friends. Here we can drink coffee, listen to music and chat about everything.

Egg: What’s your favourite time of the year? (spring – beautiful, weather – nice, warm, sunny – outdoor..,

  • I love spring because it is beautiful with green trees and flowers. The weather is very nice. It’s warm and sunny. It’s perfect for outdoor activities such as taking photos, going sightseeing. I also like summer because I love going to the beach

Egg: Where do you like to eat? (home – good/affordable – stay healthy/save money. Restaurant – food – delicious – not cook…

I like to eat at home because it is affordable and good for my health. It helps me stay healthy and save money. Sometimes I like to eat out because the food is delicious and I don’t have to cook or clean up

  1. Đưa thêm mặt đối nghịch (Vd: hỏi về lợi ích – nhưng nói cả về mặt hại)

Egg: What’s your favourite means of transport?

I like travelling by car because it is fast and convenient. I can save time and I can go everywhere. However, it is not affordable for people with low income. Sometimes, I like travelling by bus because it is cheap/affordable/economical. It helps me save money for other important things. However, it is slow and not convenient.

Egg: What’s your favourite kind of food?

I like eating fast food like pizza or bread because it is fast and convenient. I can save time. I don’t have to cook or clean up. However, I know that it is not good for my health because it can cause some health problems like cancer, obesity or heart disease.

  1. Đưa thêm thông tin về cá nhân (vd Người dân ở đất nước bạn đi lại bằng phường tiện gì – mình nói cả về cá nhân)

Egg: How do people in your city travel around?

In my city people often travel by motorike because it is fast and convenient. It helps people save time. Besides, they travel by bus because it is affordable. For me, I prefer to travel by bicycle because it is healthy and good for the environment.

Egg: Do you love animals?

Yes. I have a dog. He is 4 years old. His name is Ruby. He is really cute. I like spending time with him. It is really relaxing.

  1. Có thể đưa ý kiến người khác – cùng với ý kiến của mình (vd: Bạn thích sống ở đâu hơn? Quê hay thành phố) – Most people …. For me, I prefer…

Egg: Where do you like to live?

  • Most people like to live in big cities because it is exciting and convenient. For me, I prefer to live in the countryside. First, the air is fresh. Second, the people are friendly and kind. Last, the food is delicious and healthy

Egg: What’s your favourite room in your house?

  • Most people like their bedroom because they can do everything. For me, I like the kitchen because I like cooking and it is the place where I can share happy moments with my family

  2. What kind of music do you like?

  4. What’s your hobby?

  6. Which do you prefer, travelling by air or by train?

  7. Should fast food be imposed high taxes?

  8. Do you think smartphones are making people less social in real life?

  9. How do people in your country usually get daily news?

  10. Do you think traditional newspapers will disappear in the future? Why or why not?

  11. Tell me the last time you went on holiday?

  12. Tell me the last time you went shopping for clothes?
