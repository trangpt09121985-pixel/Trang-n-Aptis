Writing – Walking Club – phone (đáp án mới)

WALKING CLUB

Part 1: You want to join a walking club. You have 5 messages from a member of the club. Write short answers (1-5 words) to each message. Recommended time: 3 minutes.

  1. What is your name? – My surname is Nguyen./ Nguyen.

  2. Where do you live? – I live in Vietnam./ Vietnam.

  3. How many people are there in your family? – There are four people./ Four people.

  4. What is your hobby? – I like watching movies./ Watching movies.

  5. What is your favorite singer? – It is Justin Bieber./ Justin Bieber.

Part 2: You are a new member of a walking club. Fill in the form. Write in sentences. Use 20-30 words. Recommended time: 7 minutes.

Where do you often walk?

  • I often walk in the park near my house. Here I can enjoy fresh air and do some exercise. It's relaxing and beneficial for my health.

Part 3: You are communicating with other members of the club in the chat room. Reply to their questions. Write in sentences. Use 30-40 words per answer. Recommended time: 10 minutes.

A: I often go for a walk. What about you?

  • Yes, I often go for a walk too, usually in the early morning before work. It helps me feel fresh and energetic for the rest of the day, and I really enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.

B: I prefer to go to the countryside for a walk. Walking in the city is stressful and noisy. What do you think?

  • I totally agree with you. Walking in the countryside is much more relaxing, because the air is fresh and there is less traffic and noise. Walking in the city can feel stressful and unhealthy.

C: In the past, people walked long distances as part of their lives. These days, they don't walk anymore. Why?

  • I think it's because life is busier now, and people rely on cars, motorbikes and public transport to save time. Also, many people spend their free time on their phones instead of moving around.

Part 4: You are a member of the walking club. You received this email from the club's manager.

Dear members,

Nowadays, many people use their phones while walking, so they don't focus on walking. The club wants members to make suggestions on this issue.

Bests,

Club Manager.

  1. Write an email to your friend. Tell your friend about your opinion and why. Write about 50 words.

Hi Jane,

I just heard that our club wants suggestions about members using their phones while walking instead of paying attention. I think we should encourage everyone to keep their phones away during walks. What do you think? Let's talk about it over lunch.

See you later
  1. Write an email to the manager of the club. Tell the manager about your opinion and why. Write about 120-150 words.

Dear Manager,

My name is Mary. I am writing this email to express my opinions about the issue mentioned in the recent email from our club.

I have been informed that many members use their phones while walking instead of focusing on the activity. To be honest, I was quite concerned to hear that news, because it could be dangerous, especially near busy roads.

Being a member of the club, I would like to offer some ideas. First, we should remind members to keep their phones in their pockets during walks, since this is simple and easy to follow. Second, we could organize phone-free walking sessions to help members build the habit of paying full attention to their surroundings.

I hope my ideas will be useful to our club.

Best regards
